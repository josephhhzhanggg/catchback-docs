CatchBack runs a seasonal leaderboard that ranks collectors by **XP earned** during the current season. XP is earned automatically as you open packs — **$1 of pack purchases earns 1 XP** — and the same XP total drives your **VIP tier**, which unlocks real rewards as you climb.

## How XP works

XP is the progression currency on CatchBack. It is not money and can't be cashed out — it measures how much you've played this season.

- **Earn XP by opening packs** — every dollar you spend on packs earns 1 XP, across every pack type
- **Free packs don't count** — reward packs, gifts, and drops earn 0 XP
- **XP updates in real time**, and so does your leaderboard position

## Seasons

The leaderboard runs in **seasons**. Season XP resets when a new season begins, so every season is a fresh race — but the VIP status you earn is held into the following season, and members holding a VIP tier start the new season from their tier's baseline rather than zero.

## What you win

Leaderboard rank is the scoreboard; the rewards come from the **VIP tiers** your XP unlocks:

## Top Gifters

Alongside the seasonal XP board, the **Top Gifters** leaderboard recognizes the most generous members of the community — ranked by the all-time dollar value of cards and packs gifted to other members. It never resets, and it's recognition only.

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I tie with someone?">
    Collectors with equal XP share the same rank.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does my XP carry over between seasons?">
    Season XP resets when a new season begins. The VIP tier you earned is held into the following season, giving you time to re-qualify — and if you hold a tier, your new-season XP starts from that tier's baseline instead of zero.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cash out XP?">
    No. XP is progression, not currency — it can't be converted to cash, CatchCoins, or anything else. The rewards come from the VIP tiers and pack drops it unlocks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happened to the monthly CatchPoints leaderboard?">
    The old monthly leaderboard (with cash-credit payouts to the top 50) has been retired and replaced by the seasonal XP leaderboard and the VIP program. Bonus Balance credits earned from past monthly payouts remain in your wallet and are still spendable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my account is flagged for a violation?">
    Leaderboard placement and VIP rewards are for legitimate platform activity. Accounts found to be in violation of platform terms — including multi-accounting and fraudulent activity — are excluded.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
