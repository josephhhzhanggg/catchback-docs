CatchCoins are CatchBack's platform currency. You can earn them, spend them across the site, or convert them back to cash. Every CatchCoin you earn or buy is tracked in your wallet and visible in your transaction history.

## What you can do with CatchCoins

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Buy packs" icon="box">
    Use CatchCoins to purchase any pack tier — from the \$1 Base Pack all the way up to the \$10,000 Black Obsidian — or any specialty pack.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Buy on the marketplace" icon="store">
    Pay for fixed-price listings, or buy a Dutch-auction card at its current price.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Convert to cash" icon="dollar-sign">
    Withdraw what you earn from CatchBacks and marketplace sales to your bank account, or as USDC to your own crypto wallet.
  </Card>

    Earn CatchCoins through marketplace sales, promotions, and platform activity — no spending required.
  </Card>

## How to earn CatchCoins

### CatchBacks
When you sell a card back to us with its [CatchBack offer](/about-catchback/liquidity), the payout lands in your CatchCoins balance right away.

### Marketplace sales
When another collector buys one of your listings, your seller payout lands in your CatchCoins balance.

### Promotions
We occasionally credit CatchCoins for platform milestones, beta features, or campaign launches. These show up in your wallet history as adjustments.

<Note>
  Looking for referral rewards? Referrals no longer pay commission in CatchCoins — instead, you and your friend each get a **free Starter Pack**. See the [Referral Program](/about-catchback/referral-program).
</Note>

## How to withdraw CatchCoins

Only money you earned from CatchBacks and marketplace sales can be withdrawn. Funds you deposited stay on the platform for packs and purchases. The minimum withdrawal is **\$10**, and there's no conversion fee: ₡100 in CatchCoins becomes \$100 USD.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Withdraw">
    Tap your balance at the top of the screen, then switch from **Deposit** to **Withdraw**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it goes">
    Withdraw to your bank account, or out as USDC to your own crypto wallet. If you deposited by card in the last 60 days, withdrawals up to that amount can only go to a bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your funds">
    Bank withdrawals usually arrive within 1 to 3 business days, not counting weekends and holidays. USDC withdrawals usually arrive within minutes. If a withdrawal says **in review**, our team is checking it before it's sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Venmo withdrawals are no longer offered. **Store Credit** is a retired balance: packs no longer accept it, and any legacy Store Credit only appears in your history.
</Note>

## CatchCoins, Bonus Balance, and other currencies

Your wallet tracks several separate balances:

| Balance | What it is | How you get it | What you can do with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CatchCoins** | Your platform balance | Deposits, CatchBacks, marketplace sales, promos | Buy packs and marketplace listings; withdraw earnings (not deposits) |
| **Bonus Balance** (shown as **Reward Vault** in the new app design) | Reward credits from platform promotions and events | Promotional credits and reward events | Buy packs |
| **XP** | Season progression points — not money, can't be cashed out | Earned automatically on pack purchases | Climb the seasonal [leaderboard](/about-catchback/leaderboard) and unlock VIP status |

Bonus Balance credits — including payouts from past leaderboard reward events — are applied to pack purchases by default and can't be withdrawn.

## Important terms

- **No inherent value**: CatchCoins exist solely within the CatchBack platform. They are not a security, not legal tender, and not transferable outside our system except through the withdrawal flow above.
- **All purchases of CatchCoins are final**: Once CatchCoins are credited to your account, the purchase cannot be refunded or reversed.
- **Subject to platform discretion**: CatchBack reserves the right to modify CatchCoin functionality, conversion rates, and eligibility requirements at any time. We'll always communicate changes that affect existing balances before they take effect.
