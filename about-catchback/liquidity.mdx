## Up to 90% Cash Buyback

**Guaranteed buyback protection. Transform illiquid assets into liquid investments.**

CatchBack Liquidity revolutionizes the collectibles market by providing guaranteed liquidity for your trading cards. No more waiting weeks or months to find a buyer – your cards become liquid investments that can be converted to cash instantly.

## How It Works

### Guaranteed Buyback System
- **Fresh Pack Pulls**: For the first **5 minutes** after opening a pack, your pull carries an enhanced buyback offer of **90%**
- **Everything Else**: After the fresh window, every eligible card in your vault can be sold back at **80%**, any time
- **Always Available**: The standard 80% buyback doesn't expire — you can hold a card as long as you want and cash out whenever you choose

### What the offer is based on
For the first **7 days** after you acquire a card — whether you pulled it from a pack or bought it on the platform — your buyback offer is based on the card's **value at the moment you acquired it**. That basis is fixed for the window, whether the market moves up or down. After 7 days, offers are based on the card's current market value, so your offer moves with the market from then on. Your vault shows the exact dollar offer on every card.
