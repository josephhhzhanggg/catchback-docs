Before you open a CatchBack pack, you pick an **odds mode**: Standard, Chill, Hot or Classic. It's the same pack at the same price. What changes is the shape of the outcomes — how often you hit, and how big the swings are. Tap **Odds** on the pack page to switch, and the odds table updates to show exactly what you're buying.

Whichever mode you pick, a pull sells back at **100% of its value within 5 minutes**, and 80% after that. See [Liquidity](/about-catchback/liquidity).

## How the odds work

Every pack is a set of price bands — for example, \$5–\$20, \$20–\$50, and so on up to the chase. Opening a pack picks a band by its odds, then a card from that band. Standard, Chill and Hot share the same bands and change only how likely each band is. Classic keeps the original odds table.

Odds follow the inventory: they're recalculated as cards are pulled and added, and every pack page shows the table in effect before you spend a cent.

## Which packs have them

- **CatchBack's own packs, Bronze and up**, in every card game, have all four modes. So do specialty packs.
- **CatchBox watch packs** open on Classic.
- **Custom, creator and community packs don't have odds modes.** In a custom pack you set each card's odds yourself in the builder, and its pulls sell back at 90% within 5 minutes (80% after). A creator pack uses the Standard odds of the CatchBack pack at its price, and a community pack uses the odds its builder set. Each shows its odds on the pack page.

## When to pick which

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Pick Standard when…">
    - You want the default experience
    - You're not sure which mode fits your style yet
    - You're comparing packs across games and want apples-to-apples odds
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pick Chill when…">
    - You want more frequent hits and steadier results
    - You're new to pack opening and want to feel the floor before chasing ceilings
    - You're opening many packs and want smoother results overall
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pick Hot when…">
    - You're hunting for chase cards and willing to accept more floor pulls in exchange
    - You enjoy the swings — bigger wins, more misses
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pick Classic when…">
    - You liked the odds from before odds modes, and want to keep playing them
    - You want to buy add-ons with your pack
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## A few things to know

- **You pick a mode per opening.** Open a Chill Bronze now, a Hot Gold later — no commitment.
- **Standard, Chill and Hot open one pack at a time.** Classic can open several at once.
- **Battles use Standard odds**, so every seat rolls the same table.
