### Pack Building Process

#### Step 1: Select Your Cards
Choose the specific cards you want to appear in your pack from our authenticated database.

#### Step 2: Set Your Odds and Pack Price
Every card starts with an equal chance, and you can adjust each card's pull probability yourself using the odds sliders. The pack price adjusts automatically as you modify the odds — weight your favorite card higher and the price rises to match; spread the odds out and it falls.

<Note>
  Custom packs don't use the Chill / Standard / Hot risk levels — those apply to our standard pack tiers (see [Risk Levels](/about-catchback/risk-levels)). In a custom pack, the per-card odds you set *are* the risk profile.
</Note>

#### Step 3: Open Your Pack
Once you're satisfied with your card selection and odds, open your pack and see what you get!

> **Note:** The pack opening animation and your card result are determined at the moment you open the pack. In rare cases, network issues during the animation may cause a different card image to appear than the one actually pulled. Your actual result is always recorded server-side at the time of opening—if you notice a discrepancy, contact support and we'll verify your true pull.

## What Makes Our Packs Transparent?

### Complete Odds Disclosure
Unlike traditional packs where odds are hidden or vague, every pack you buy or build and buy shows:
- **Exact pull rates** for every possible card or item

### Real-Time Optimization System

Our real-time optimization system continuously monitors live market values and automatically sorts cards into value buckets (P0-P5). A \$45 card in the P1 bucket (\$40-50) moves to P2 (\$50-100) if its price increases, ensuring probability tables always reflect current market reality.

The system uses **[machine learning optimization](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.html)** to minimize the difference between expected value and pack price. It heavily penalizes scenarios where expected value is less than price, then iteratively adjusts probabilities until optimal.

Result: Pack probabilities automatically adapt to market conditions while guaranteeing you never pay more than expected value. For technical implementation details, see our [Provably Fair](/about-catchback/provably-fair) page.
