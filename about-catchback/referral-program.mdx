---
title: "Referral Program"
description: "Invite friends to CatchBack — when they redeem your code, you both get a free Starter Pack"
---

Every CatchBack account comes with a unique referral code. Share it with friends — when a friend redeems your code, **you both receive a free Starter Pack**, delivered unopened to each of your vaults. No spending required on either side.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your referral code">
    Open the Referrals screen in the app (or your referrals page on the web) — your unique code is displayed there, along with a shareable invite link. Tap to copy the code or send the link directly to friends.
  </Step>

  <Step title="A friend redeems your code">
    Your friend enters your code in the app, or signs up through your invite link. Redemption is a one-time step — each account can redeem a referral code once.
  </Step>

  <Step title="You both get a free Starter Pack">
    The moment the code is redeemed, a free Starter Pack lands in **both** vaults — one for you, one for your friend. You'll get a notification when yours arrives.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open your packs">
    Open your Starter Pack whenever you're ready. Whatever you pull is yours to keep in the vault, ship to yourself, or collect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you both get

- **A free Starter Pack each** — the same Starter Pack sold on the platform, granted unopened to your vault
- **No limit for referrers** — every friend who redeems your code earns you another free pack
- Your friend gets their own referral code too, so they can start inviting people right away

<Note>
  Cards pulled from free referral packs are yours to keep, vault, or ship — but because you didn't pay for the pack, they aren't eligible for the CatchBack cash buyback.
</Note>

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can redeem a referral code?">
    Any account that hasn't been referred before. Each account can redeem exactly one referral code, ever — and you can't redeem your own.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?">
    No. You receive a free Starter Pack for every friend who redeems your code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do the packs show up?">
    Immediately. Both packs are granted the moment the code is redeemed, and both of you are notified. Look for yours in your vault's packs section.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sell back cards from my free referral pack?">
    No — pulls from free referral packs aren't eligible for the CatchBack buyback. You can keep them in the vault or ship them to yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I refer myself with a second account?">
    No. Self-referral and multi-accounting violate platform terms, and rewards gained that way will be reversed, with potential account consequences.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happened to the old commission program?">
    The earlier program, which paid referrers an ongoing commission on referee purchases, has been retired. The current program replaces it: both parties now receive a free Starter Pack at redemption.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Important terms

- **One redemption per account.** The referral relationship is established once, when the code is redeemed.
- **Eligibility is at platform discretion.** Accounts found to be in violation of platform terms — including multi-accounting and fraudulent activity — are excluded from referral rewards.
- **The program can change.** CatchBack reserves the right to modify referral rewards and eligibility going forward. Rewards already granted are yours to keep.
