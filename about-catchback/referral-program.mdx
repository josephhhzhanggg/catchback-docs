---
title: "Referral Program"
description: "Invite friends to CatchBack — you both get a free Starter Pack"
---

Every CatchBack account comes with a unique referral code. Share it with friends — **you both receive a free Starter Pack**, delivered unopened to your vaults. Your friend gets theirs as soon as they redeem your code, and you get yours once they've deposited \$10 in total.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your referral code">
    In the app, go to the **Community** tab and tap **REFERRALS** at the top left. On the website, go to **Portfolio**, then **Referrals**. Your unique code is displayed there, along with a shareable invite link. Tap to copy the code or send the link directly to friends.
  </Step>

  <Step title="A friend redeems your code">
    Your friend enters your code in the app, or signs up through your invite link. They need a verified phone number on their account to redeem. Redemption is a one-time step — each account can redeem a referral code once.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your friend gets a free Starter Pack">
    The moment the code is redeemed, a free Starter Pack lands in your friend's vault.
  </Step>

  <Step title="You get yours after their first \$10">
    Once your friend has deposited \$10 in total, a free Starter Pack lands in your vault. You'll get a notification when it arrives.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open your packs">
    Open your Starter Pack whenever you're ready. Whatever you pull is yours to keep in the vault, ship to yourself, or collect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you both get

- **A free Starter Pack each** — the same Starter Pack sold on the platform, granted unopened to your vault
- **No limit for referrers** — every friend who redeems your code and deposits \$10 earns you another free pack
- Your friend gets their own referral code too, so they can start inviting people right away

<Note>
  Cards pulled from free referral packs are yours to keep, vault, or ship — but because you didn't pay for the pack, they aren't eligible for the CatchBack cash buyback.
</Note>

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can redeem a referral code?">
    Any account that hasn't been referred before and has a verified phone number. Each account can redeem exactly one referral code, ever, and only one account per email inbox can use referral rewards (email aliases of the same inbox count as one). You can't redeem your own code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?">
    No. You receive a free Starter Pack for every friend who redeems your code and deposits \$10 in total.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do the packs show up?">
    Your friend's pack arrives the moment they redeem your code. Yours arrives once they've deposited \$10 in total, and you're notified when it does. Look for it in your vault's packs section.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sell back cards from my free referral pack?">
    No — pulls from free referral packs aren't eligible for the CatchBack buyback. You can keep them in the vault or ship them to yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I refer myself with a second account?">
    No. Self-referral and multi-accounting violate platform terms, and rewards gained that way will be reversed, with potential account consequences.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happened to the old commission program?">
    The earlier program, which paid referrers an ongoing commission on referee purchases, has been retired. The current program replaces it: both parties now receive a free Starter Pack.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Important terms

- **One redemption per account and per email inbox.** The referral relationship is established once, when the code is redeemed.
- **Eligibility is at platform discretion.** Accounts found to be in violation of platform terms — including multi-accounting and fraudulent activity — are excluded from referral rewards.
- **The program can change.** CatchBack reserves the right to modify referral rewards and eligibility going forward. Rewards already granted are yours to keep.
