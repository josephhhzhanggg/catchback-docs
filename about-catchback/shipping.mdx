# Shipping at CatchBack

Every card in your vault can be shipped to you whenever you want it in hand. We make it simple, safe, and affordable with professional packaging, real carrier tracking, and optional insurance.

## What shipping costs

### United States

| Order | Standard shipping |
|---|---|
| Cards totaling **$50+** in market value | **Free** |
| Cards totaling under $50 | **$8 flat** |

At checkout you can also upgrade to a faster carrier service — we quote live rates from major carriers and you pay the quoted rate, with no markup added.

### Optional add-ons

- **Shipping insurance** — insure your shipment for its full market value for **2% of the cards' value** (minimum $1)
- **Signature confirmation** — available on supported carrier services for a small pass-through surcharge

## International shipping

We ship internationally to a growing list of supported countries. A few things work differently outside the US:

- **You choose a carrier rate at checkout** — international orders are quoted live and paid at the carrier's rate. The free-shipping tier applies to US orders only.
- **Customs declarations** are prepared automatically from your order.
- **Very high-value international orders** can't ship on the automated path — they're recorded as a shipping request and our team contacts you to arrange delivery directly.

## Our shipping process

- **Professional handling**: Your cards are packaged with care by our team, with protective materials suited to slabs and raw cards
- **Full tracking**: Every shipment gets a tracking number with real-time updates from pickup to delivery
- **Trusted carriers**: We ship through established national carriers with proven track records
- **Secure packaging**: Multiple layers of protection, weather resistance, and tamper-evident sealing

## Tracking & updates

Stay informed throughout the shipping process:

- **Real-time tracking**: Monitor your package every step of the way
- **Delivery notifications**: Get alerts when your package arrives
- **Support access**: Our team is available if you have any questions — [contact support](/resources/support)
