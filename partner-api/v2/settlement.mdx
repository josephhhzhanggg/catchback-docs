title: "Paying through you"
description: "Partner settlement: buy packs for your customers by your own id for them, keep their cards in a CatchBack vault, and settle orders, sell-backs and shipping on one statement with a credit limit."
---

Choose **Paying through you** in Get started when your customers have no crypto wallet. You buy for them and charge them however you already do. We keep each card in a CatchBack vault for that customer, and everything they do (orders, sell-backs, shipping) goes on one statement between you and us.

|  | Wallet model | Paying through you |
|---|---|---|
| Who pays CatchBack | Each customer, in USDC | You, on your statement |
| A customer is | A wallet address | Your own id for them |
| Their cards | NFTs in their wallet | In their CatchBack vault, read with the API |
| Sell-backs | USDC to their wallet | Credited to your statement |
| Shipping | Paid by the holder | On your statement |
| Revenue share | Paid to your USDC address | Netted on your statement |

The endpoints for catalog, odds, orders, webhooks and keys are the same. Wallet fields (`recipient`, `refund_address`, token ids, quotes and burns) are not used, and an order that sends one is refused with `WALLET_FIELDS_NOT_ACCEPTED`.

## Your customers

Every call names a customer by `customer`: your id for them, 1-128 characters of `A-Z a-z 0-9 _ . : -`. Use something stable that is not personal data (your internal user id, never an email address or phone number).

The first order for a new id creates that customer's CatchBack account; later orders with the same id reach the same account. Ids are yours alone: the same id in another partner's program is a different customer. You never see or send our account ids.

## Buy a pack: `POST /orders`

```bash
curl -X POST "$CATCHBACK_BASE_URL/api/partner/v2/orders" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-7f3a91" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"pack_type":"bronze","category":"pokemon","odds":"standard","quantity":2,"customer":"user-1842"}'
```

There is no payment step: the order is charged to your statement when it is accepted, and its packs open right away. The response is the [order](/partner-api/v2/orders) with `custody: "vault"` and `customer`, and each pack's status ends at `in_vault` instead of `minted`. A retry with the same `Idempotency-Key` returns the same order and is never charged twice.

An order is refused, and nothing is charged, when:

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `402` | `CREDIT_LIMIT` | It would take what you owe past your credit limit. The error carries `owed`, `credit_limit` and `order_total`. |
| `429` | `CUSTOMER_DAILY_LIMIT` | This customer has spent their limit for the UTC day. Carries `spent_today`, `limit` and `retry_after_seconds`. |
| `429` | `DAILY_LIMIT` | Your program has spent its daily limit, if it has one. Same fields. |
| `403` | `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED` | We can't sell to this customer. Don't retry. |
| `400` | `BAD_CUSTOMER` | `customer` is missing or not 1-128 of the allowed characters. |

## Their vault: `GET /customers/{customer}/vault`

The cards the customer holds now, newest first, each with what it sells back for at this moment:

```json
{
  "customer": "user-1842",
  "cards": [
    {
      "card": { "id": "838a5e7f-…", "category": "pokemon", "name": "Charizard ex PSA 10" },
      "order_id": "order-7f3a91",
      "pulled_at": "2026-11-24T18:02:11Z",
      "value_at_pull": 31,
      "sellback": { "amount": 31, "rate": 1, "fresh": true, "locked_until": "2026-12-01T18:02:11Z" }
    }
  ],
  "truncated": false
}
```

`sellback.fresh` is true while the card is in its fresh window after the pull, at the higher rate. For 7 days after a card is acquired its sell-back is based on its value when acquired (`locked_until`), so a price change in that time doesn't move the offer. A sold or shipped card leaves the vault. `truncated: true` means the customer has more pulls than one read covers.

## Sell back: `POST /customers/{customer}/cards/{card_id}/sellback`

```bash
curl -X POST "$CATCHBACK_BASE_URL/api/partner/v2/customers/user-1842/cards/838a5e7f-…/sellback" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"min_payout": 30}'
```

Sells the card at its current offer and credits the payout to your statement; nothing is paid out to the customer, so you credit them your way. `min_payout` (optional) refuses an offer below it. A card sells once: a retry returns the first sale with `status: "already_sold"`.

```json
{ "sellback": { "status": "sold", "transaction_id": "…", "payout_amount": 31, "payout_rate": 1, "on_statement": true } }
```

## Ship: estimate, then create

1. [`POST /customers/{customer}/shipments/estimate`](#ship-estimate-then-create) with `card_ids` (from the vault) and an `address` returns `rates`, each with a `carrier`, `service`, `token` and `amount`. Add `cover_amount` to price shipping insurance.
2. `POST /customers/{customer}/shipments` with the same `card_ids` and `address`, the chosen `rate: { carrier, token }`, and `max_cost`, plus an `Idempotency-Key`. The postage (and any cover) goes on your statement; past your credit limit it is refused with `402 CREDIT_LIMIT`. The response is the shipment, with its `id`.
3. Follow it with [`nft_shipment.updated`](/partner-api/v2/webhooks#events) or `GET /customers/{customer}/shipments` (the customer's latest 100, newest first). `status` moves `processing` → `shipped` (with `carrier` and `tracking_number`) → `delivered`.

A rate that rose above `max_cost` since the estimate is refused with `PRICE_CHANGED`, and a card that left the vault meanwhile with `CARDS_NOT_SHIPPABLE`. A shipment that doesn't go through is taken off your statement.

## Your statement: `GET /settlement`

What you owe now, your limits, and one month of entries (`period=YYYY-MM`, the current UTC month by default):

```json
{
  "owed": 236.65,
  "credit_limit": 10000,
  "available": 9763.35,
  "customer_daily_limit": 500,
  "daily_limit": null,
  "period": {
    "month": "2026-11", "from": "2026-11-01T00:00:00Z", "to": "2026-12-01T00:00:00Z",
    "opening_balance": 0, "closing_balance": 236.65,
    "totals": { "order": { "amount": 250, "entries": 2 }, "sellback": { "amount": -31, "entries": 1 }, "shipping": { "amount": 17.65, "entries": 1 } }
  },
  "entries": [
    { "id": "…", "kind": "sellback", "amount": -31, "created_at": "…", "customer": "user-1842", "order_id": "…", "card_id": "838a5e7f-…", "sellback_id": "…" }
  ],
  "next_cursor": null
}
```

A positive `amount` adds to what you owe, a negative one takes from it. The kinds are `order`, `order_refund`, `shipping`, `shipping_refund`, `sellback`, `revenue_share`, `payment` and `adjustment`. Page with `limit` (up to 200) and `cursor`. Your manager's **Statement** page shows the same, with a CSV export.

Your revenue share is netted on the statement, and you settle the balance as agreed with us. Your credit limit and daily limits are set by CatchBack; ask your contact to change them.

## Webhooks

The events are the same as the wallet model's, with `customer` instead of a wallet:

- `nft_order.updated` as an order's packs open into the vault.
- `sellback.updated` when a sell-back is credited (`status: "credited"`, `amount`, `card_id`, `statement_entry_id`).
- `nft_shipment.updated` as a shipment moves.
