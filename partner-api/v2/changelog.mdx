title: "Changelog"
description: "What changed in Partner API v2, newest first."
---

Changes to `v2` are additive: a response can gain fields, an enum can gain values, and a new endpoint can appear, so build your parser to ignore what it doesn't know. Dates are US Pacific time.

<Update label="November 24, 2026" description="Fixes from sandbox testing">
  - Non-crypto model: an order that sends any wallet field (`recipient`, `refund_address`, `partner_user_ref`, `holder`, `payout_chain` or `payout_address`) is now refused with `WALLET_FIELDS_NOT_ACCEPTED`. Before, only `recipient` and `refund_address` were refused and the rest were ignored.
  - Non-crypto model: sell-back retries sent at the same moment now all return the first sale (`already_sold`) instead of an error, and a UUID `card_id` matches in any case. A refused sale with no code of its own answers `SELLBACK_REFUSED`.
  - Non-crypto model: a customer shipment's first `nft_shipment.updated` now arrives when it is created (`processing`), not first at `shipped`.
  - Crypto Wallet model: `nft_shipment.updated` now carries `shipment_id`, and when a multi-token box moves every token burned into it gets the event, with the box's `shipping_cost`.
  - Crypto Wallet model: a token with a paid shipment waiting for its burn can't be sold back: the quote and the burn answer `409 TOKEN_IN_SHIPMENT`. A token can have only one paid shipment at a time.
  - Crypto Wallet model: the zero address is refused as a `recipient`, `refund_address` or `payout_address`.
  - Sandbox: `POST /sandbox/payments/{intentId}` refuses an `amount` that isn't a positive number with `400 BAD_AMOUNT`.
  - Crypto Wallet model: a mixed-case address must carry a valid EIP-55 checksum (all-lowercase still works), and invisible characters are refused. A bad `partner_user_ref` is refused with `BAD_PARTNER_USER_REF` instead of being dropped.
  - Crypto Wallet model: selling back a token that was just burned answers `409 TOKEN_NOT_LIVE` (quote) or `409 TOKEN_BURNED` (burn) instead of a chain error, and burning a token expires its other open quotes.
  - Crypto Wallet model: a one-token shipment paid short shows `underpaid: { received, due }`.
  - Calls for the other model are refused clearly: a token, wallet or quote endpoint on a Non-crypto program answers `400 NOT_WALLET_PROGRAM`, and `customer` on a Crypto Wallet order answers `400 NOT_PARTNER_SETTLED`.
  - The pool lists every odds choice a pack sells, with `available: false` on one that isn't on sale. A batch's units list in unit order on `GET /orders`, a forged cursor answers `400 BAD_CURSOR`, and timestamps all end in `Z`.
  - US shipping addresses need a real state or territory code.
  - Sandbox: a shipment moves only `processing` → `shipped` → `delivered` (`409 BAD_TRANSITION` otherwise); resending its status is a no-op.
  - MCP: an argument a tool doesn't take answers `UNKNOWN_ARGUMENT`, a wrong type `BAD_ARGUMENT`, and `read_doc` on a missing page `DOC_NOT_FOUND`.
  - The [OpenAPI spec](/partner-api/v2/openapi.yaml) now lists every error code, including the MCP server's own (see [Errors](/partner-api/v2/errors#mcp-server-codes)), and allows `singles` as an item's category.
</Update>

<Update label="November 24, 2026" description="Non-crypto model">
  - New page: [Integration models](/partner-api/v2/integration-models) lays out the two models side by side, with an architecture diagram for each, where the money goes, what you build and which endpoints each uses. The docs now call them the **Crypto Wallet model** and the **Non-crypto model**, as your manager does (`payment_mode` `wallet` and `partner`).
  - Customer shipment requests now return a `code` for every bad field: `BAD_CARD_IDS`, `BAD_ADDRESS` (with `fields`), `BAD_COVER`, `BAD_RATE` and `BAD_MAX_COST`.
  - New payment model: [Non-crypto model](/partner-api/v2/settlement). For customers without a crypto wallet, you buy for them by your own id for them (`customer`), their cards stay in a CatchBack vault, and orders, sell-backs and shipping settle on one statement with a credit limit.
  - New endpoints for these programs: `GET /customers/{customer}/vault`, `POST /customers/{customer}/cards/{card_id}/sellback`, `POST /customers/{customer}/shipments/estimate`, `POST /customers/{customer}/shipments`, `GET /customers/{customer}/shipments` and `GET /settlement`.
  - `POST /orders` takes `customer` on these programs and refuses wallet fields (`WALLET_FIELDS_NOT_ACCEPTED`); new error codes `CREDIT_LIMIT` (402), `CUSTOMER_DAILY_LIMIT` and `DAILY_LIMIT` (429, with `retry_after_seconds`). Orders gain `custody` and `customer`, and the status `in_vault`.
  - In the sandbox, `POST /sandbox/shipments/{id}` also takes a customer shipment's `id`.
  - The [MCP server](/partner-api/v2/mcp) has these as tools: `get_customer_vault`, `sell_back_customer_card`, `estimate_customer_shipment`, `create_customer_shipment`, `list_customer_shipments` and `get_settlement`, and `create_order` takes `customer`.
  - `GET /keys` (`whoami`) now returns your program's `payment_mode`: `wallet` or `partner`.
</Update>

<Update label="November 23, 2026" description="One-tap shipping">
  - Ship one token in one tap: where your program supports it, [Create a one-token shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-token-shipment) returns `payment.method: "signature"` and a `sign` message. The holder signs it once, you send it to [Burn without gas](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/relay-burn) as `permit_signature`, and the postage and the burn land in one transaction. No separate payment, and no 24-hour burn window. See [One tap](/partner-api/v2/shipping#one-tap-pay-and-burn-together).
  - New: `payment_method` on one-token shipments (`signature` or `checkout`); `permit_signature` on the burn endpoint; error codes `BAD_PAYMENT_METHOD`, `SIGNATURE_PAYMENT_UNAVAILABLE`, `POSTAGE_DUE`, `BAD_PERMIT_SIGNATURE`, `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` and `PERMIT2_NOT_APPROVED`.
  - Multi-token shipments are unchanged: pay first, then burn.
</Update>

<Update label="November 22, 2026" description="Self-serve sandbox; sandbox keys start cb_test_; order lists by ref">
  - Sandbox keys now start `cb_test_`, so a key says where it works: `cb_test_` keys at `https://sandbox.catchbackcards.com`, `cb_live_` keys at `https://catchbackcards.com`. Production refuses a sandbox key with `401` and the code `SANDBOX_KEY`. Sandbox keys made earlier start `cb_live_` and keep working in the sandbox. See [Authentication and keys](/partner-api/v2/authentication#sandbox-and-production-keys).
  - The [MCP server](/partner-api/v2/mcp) now opens by telling the assistant which environment it is connected to, and where a sandbox key's direct API calls go.
  - Sandbox payouts and refunds are now real Base Sepolia transactions in CatchBack Sandbox USDC (`sbxUSDC`), a test token worth nothing, so a `sent` payout has a `tx_hash` you can open on BaseScan. See the [Sandbox](/partner-api/v2/quickstart).
  - The sandbox now names test networks for its simulated money: payouts, refunds and deposit addresses are on `evm:84532` (Base Sepolia) and `solana:devnet`, never a mainnet. It still accepts `evm:8453` and `solana:mainnet` in a request, as those test networks.
  - The [OpenAPI spec](/partner-api/v2/openapi.yaml) marks the operations that move real money in production with `x-moves-money: true`: create an order, create a sell-back quote, burn without gas, and both ways to create a shipment. In the sandbox they move only simulated money.
  - Set up your sandbox yourself: Get started in your manager has **Set up my sandbox**, then you create a sandbox key there. See the [Sandbox](/partner-api/v2/quickstart).
  - [List orders](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-orders) with `partner_user_ref` now matches the orders you sent with that ref, whichever wallet they went to. `TOO_MANY_WALLETS` is gone.
  - [List packs](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-packs) and [Get a pack's odds and pool](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-pack-pool) can be up to a minute old; stock is checked again when you order.
  - [Webhooks](/partner-api/v2/webhooks): send test events, see payloads, retry deliveries and rotate the signing secret from your manager.
</Update>

<Update label="November 19, 2026" description="Burn without gas">
  - New: [Burn without gas](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/relay-burn), `POST /tokens/{tokenId}/burn`. On EVM programs the holder signs the sell-back or shipment quote instead of sending a transaction, and we send the burn and pay its gas. See [Sell-back](/partner-api/v2/sell-back#evm-without-gas).
  - The contract gains `burnWithQuoteFor(quote, signature, holderSignature)`. `burnWithQuote` is unchanged.
</Update>

<Update label="November 17, 2026" description="Partner API v2: wallets & NFTs">
  - New: Partner API v2 under `/api/partner/v2`, scope `nft:orders`. Each customer pays for their own pack in USDC on Base or Solana, and each pulled item is delivered as an NFT to the customer's own wallet. See the [overview](/partner-api/v2/overview).
  - Packs and orders: [List packs](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-packs), [Get a pack's odds and pool](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-pack-pool) with the Standard, Hot, Chill and Classic [odds](/partner-api/v2/odds), [Create an order](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-order) for 1-10 packs, [List orders](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-orders), [Get an order](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-order) and its [provably-fair proof](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-order-verification).
  - Tokens and sell-back: [Wallet tokens](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-wallet-tokens), [Get a token](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-token) with its sell-back schedule, [sell-back quotes](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-sellback-quote) to burn with, paid in USDC, and [Get a quote](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-quote) / [List quotes](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-quotes).
  - Shipping: one token or up to 25 in one box, paid once, burned per token, with renewal and carrier tracking. See [Shipping](/partner-api/v2/shipping).
  - Self-serve [webhooks](/partner-api/v2/webhooks): `nft_order.updated`, `sellback.updated`, `nft_shipment.updated` and `ping`.
  - Keys: [read](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-key), [rotate](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/rotate-key) and [revoke the previous key](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/revoke-previous-key).
  - [Revenue statement](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-revenue), a [sandbox](/partner-api/v2/quickstart) with simulated payments on a public testnet, and keyless [token metadata](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-token-metadata).
  - v2 replaces v1: the v1 Partner API (a prefunded balance, balance-paid purchases, withdrawals) is retired for every partner.
</Update>
