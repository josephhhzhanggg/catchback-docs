## Request rate

Per key:

| Endpoint | Limit |
|---|---|
| `POST /orders`, `POST /customers/{customer}/cards/{card_id}/sellback` and `POST /customers/{customer}/shipments`, together | 30 a minute |
| `POST /shipments/estimate`, `POST /tokens/{tokenId}/shipment/estimate` and `POST /customers/{customer}/shipments/estimate`, together | 30 a minute |
| Every other endpoint, together | 120 a minute |

Over a limit is a `429 RATE_LIMITED` with `retry_after_seconds` and a `Retry-After` header. Treat these numbers as ceilings and stay well under them.

```json
{ "error": "Too many requests. Slow down.", "code": "RATE_LIMITED", "retry_after_seconds": 12 }
```

## Unpaid packs

Crypto Wallet model, per wallet, in your program (a multi-pack order counts each pack):

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Unpaid packs ordered in the last 10 minutes | 30 |
| Unpaid packs open at once | 100 (unpaid orders expire after 24 hours) |

An order that would go over either is a `429 TOO_MANY_UNPAID_ORDERS`, with `unpaid_packs`, `limit`, `retry_after_seconds` and a `Retry-After` header, and creates nothing. Paying an open order frees it at once, so a customer who pays as they go never meets these. Paid orders never count.

## Money limits

| Limit | Value | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Sell-back payouts per address | **$5,000 per UTC day**, per payout address | A payout that would take the USDC sent to that address that day over the limit is `held` for review by our team, then released |
| Sell-back payouts per program | A daily cap across all your customers (set with you at onboarding) | Beyond it, payouts that day are `held` for review, then released. A safety net against pricing errors or a compromised integration; normal volume never reaches it |
| Underpayment | 1% tolerance | A payment more than 1% short opens nothing: the order goes to `refund_due` with `failure_reason: "underpaid"` and everything that arrived is refunded |

There is no limit on how much a wallet buys and pays for; only unpaid packs are capped (above).

### Non-crypto model

| Limit | Value | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Credit limit | **$10,000** to start, set by CatchBack | What you owe (orders and postage, less sell-back credits and your share) can't pass it: an order or shipment that would is refused with `402 CREDIT_LIMIT` and charges nothing |
| Per customer | **$500 per UTC day** by default | Beyond it, that customer's orders are refused with `429 CUSTOMER_DAILY_LIMIT` until the next UTC day (`retry_after_seconds`) |
| Per program | Optional daily cap, set with you | Beyond it, orders are refused with `429 DAILY_LIMIT` until the next UTC day |
| Customer shipments | **50 per key per UTC day** | Beyond it, `429 DAILY_SHIPMENT_LIMIT_REACHED` until the next UTC day |

The daily limits count every order accepted that UTC day. An order refused at the door (any of these limits, or `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED`) counts for nothing, but a pack that was accepted and then couldn't open still counts, even though its price is taken back off your statement.

Ask your CatchBack contact to change them. [`GET /settlement`](/partner-api/v2/settlement) always shows your current limits and what's available.

## Sanctions screening

Every wallet is checked against the US Treasury (OFAC) sanctions list of digital-currency addresses:

- the `recipient` and `refund_address` of an order;
- the `holder` and `payout_address` of a sell-back quote;
- the `holder` of a shipment;
- every payout destination, again right before we send.

A match is a `403 WALLET_REFUSED` (or a payout `held`). If screening is briefly unavailable we fail closed: `503 SCREENING_UNAVAILABLE` on a request, or a payout `held` until it can be checked.

Your servers can call from anywhere except a country on our sanctions list, which gets a `403` `Access denied` on every endpoint.

## KYC

KYC of your users is yours, per our agreement. Never send personal data in `partner_user_ref`.

## Held payouts

A payout is `held` when it is over the daily payout limit, when its destination fails or can't complete screening, or when payouts are paused. Held payouts are reviewed and released by our team; the quote stays `redeemed` and the amount owed doesn't change. Show a held payout to your customer as "under review", not failed.
