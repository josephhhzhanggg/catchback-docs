---
title: "Create a customer shipment"
api: "POST /api/partner/v2/customers/{customer}/shipments"
description: "Paying through you: ship 1-10 of a customer's vault cards in one box, with the postage on your statement."
---

Scope: `nft:orders` · Rate limit: 30 a minute, shared with [Create an order](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-order) and [Sell back a customer's card](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/sell-back-customer-card)

For [Paying through you](/partner-api/v2/settlement) programs only; a wallet program gets `400 NOT_PARTNER_SETTLED`.

Ships 1-10 of a customer's vault cards in one box at a rate from [Estimate a customer shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/estimate-customer-shipment). We re-quote the rate; the postage (and any cover's premium) goes on your statement, and nothing is paid or signed. A shipment that doesn't go through is taken off your statement.

The response is the shipment (`processing`) with its `id`. It then moves to `shipped` (with `carrier` and `tracking_number`) and `delivered`: follow it with [List a customer's shipments](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-customer-shipments) or the [`nft_shipment.updated`](/partner-api/v2/webhooks#events) webhook. Each key may create up to 50 shipments a UTC day.

<Warning>
  Always send an `Idempotency-Key`, and reuse it when retrying. A retry with the same key never charges twice. See [Idempotency](/partner-api/v2/idempotency).
</Warning>

<ParamField header="Idempotency-Key" type="string" required>
  Your own shipment id: 8-128 characters of `A-Za-z0-9_-`. The same key with a different request (`card_ids`, `address`, `rate`, `cover_amount`) is `422`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="customer" type="string" required>
  Your own id for the customer.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="card_ids" type="string[]" required>
  1-10 distinct `card.id`s from the customer's vault, as for the estimate.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="address" type="object" required>
  As for [Estimate a customer shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/estimate-customer-shipment).
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="rate" type="object" required>
  `{ "carrier": "...", "token": "..." }` from the estimate's `rates`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="max_cost" type="number" required>
  The most the shipping may cost, in dollars: the estimate rate's `total` if you sent `cover_amount`, else its `amount`. If the re-quote is higher, nothing is created and you get `PRICE_CHANGED` with the current `amount`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="cover_amount" type="number | null">
  Shipping insurance cover in dollars, as for the estimate.
</ParamField>

## Response

**201** (200 with `"idempotent_replay": true` on a replay, with the shipment as it stands now). The shipment is the whole body, not wrapped.

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">The shipment. In the sandbox, pass it to [Simulate shipping](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/sandbox-ship).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string">`processing` once created; then `shipped` and `delivered`. Also `pending` (being created), `cancelled` (by our team, rare; contact us) and `needs_review` (we couldn't confirm it; our team resolves it).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="card_ids" type="string[]" />
<ResponseField name="carrier" type="string | null" />
<ResponseField name="service" type="string | null" />
<ResponseField name="service_token" type="string | null">The rate's `token`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="cost" type="number | null">What went on your statement: the postage plus any cover premium.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="cover_amount" type="number | null" />
<ResponseField name="insurance_cost" type="number | null">The cover's premium (0 without cover).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="destination_country" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="tracking_number" type="string | null">Set once shipped.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="tracking_url" type="string | null">The carrier's own tracking page.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="created_at" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="status_updated_at" type="string | null" />
<ResponseField name="shipped_at" type="string | null" />
<ResponseField name="expected_delivery" type="string | null">A `YYYY-MM-DD` date.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="delivered_at" type="string | null" />
<ResponseField name="idempotent_replay" type="boolean">Present and `true` on a replay.</ResponseField>

We never return the delivery address.

## Errors

Nothing is created or charged on any refusal below, except `SHIPMENT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`.

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `BAD_IDEMPOTENCY_KEY` | `Idempotency-Key` missing or malformed |
| `400` | | Any `400` of [Estimate a customer shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/estimate-customer-shipment#errors); or `rate` isn't a rate from the estimate, or `max_cost` is missing, not positive or over 10,000 |
| `402` | `CREDIT_LIMIT` | The postage would take what you owe past your credit limit; the body has `owed`, `credit_limit` and `shipment_total` |
| `403` | `CUSTOMER_BLOCKED` | This customer may not ship cards |
| `403` | `CUSTOMER_UNAVAILABLE` | This customer's account is unavailable |
| `404` | `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND` | No customer with that id has bought from your program |
| `409` | `CARDS_NOT_IN_VAULT` | A card isn't in this customer's vault from your program; `card_ids` lists them |
| `409` | `CARDS_NOT_SHIPPABLE` | A card can't ship, or left the vault while the shipment was being created |
| `409` | `RATE_UNAVAILABLE` | The carrier no longer offers that rate; estimate again |
| `409` | `COVER_UNAVAILABLE` | The chosen service has no cover option; choose another rate or leave `cover_amount` out |
| `409` | `PRICE_CHANGED` | The rate (with any cover) now costs more than `max_cost`; the body has the current `amount` |
| `409` | `IDEMPOTENCY_IN_PROGRESS` | A shipment with this key is still being created; wait, then retry with the same key |
| `409` | `IDEMPOTENCY_RETRY` | An earlier attempt with this key just failed and charged nothing; retry with the same key |
| `422` | `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` | The key was already used for a different shipment |
| `429` | `DAILY_SHIPMENT_LIMIT_REACHED` | This key has created 50 shipments this UTC day; the body has `limit` and `resets_at` |
| `500` | `SHIPMENT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` | We couldn't confirm whether it went through; it is with our team. Retrying with the same key is safe and never charges twice; don't resend under a new key |
| `502` | | We couldn't confirm the carrier rate; retry |
| `503` | `SHIPMENT_NOT_CREATED` | It didn't go through, and its postage is being taken off your statement |
| `503` | | It didn't go through and nothing was charged, or a check couldn't run; retry with the same key |
