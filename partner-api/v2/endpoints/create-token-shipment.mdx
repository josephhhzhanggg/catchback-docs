---
title: "Create a one-token shipment"
api: "POST /api/partner/v2/tokens/{tokenId}/shipment"
description: "Create a shipment of one token: one tap (the holder signs once to pay and burn) where supported, or pay first."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

<Tip>
  **One tap.** Where your program supports it, `payment.method` is `"signature"` (the default there): the holder signs `payment.sign` once, and you send that to [Burn without gas](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/relay-burn) as `permit_signature`. It pays the postage and burns together. See [One tap](/partner-api/v2/shipping#one-tap-pay-and-burn-together).
</Tip>

The same as [Create a shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-shipment), for one token: it takes the same body without `token_ids` and answers with the shipment as `quote` (no `shipment_id`, no `tokens`: the token's own `quote_id` names the shipment). Once paid, [Get a shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-shipment) with the `quote_id` returns what the holder burns with; the burn ships the item.

<Warning>
  Always send an `Idempotency-Key`, and reuse it when retrying. See [Idempotency](/partner-api/v2/idempotency).
</Warning>

<ParamField path="tokenId" type="string" required>
  The token's decimal `token_id`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField header="Idempotency-Key" type="string" required>
  8-128 characters of `A-Za-z0-9_-`, unique within your program. Shared key space with [Create a shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-shipment). A replay must send the same `holder`, `address`, `rate` and `cover_amount`; `max_cost` and `payment_method` aren't compared.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="holder" type="string" required>
  The wallet that holds the token now. Checked on chain.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="address" type="object" required>
  As for [Estimate a shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/estimate-shipment).
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="rate" type="object" required>
  `{ "carrier": "...", "token": "..." }` from an estimate's `rates`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="payment_method" type="string">
  `"signature"`: one tap, pay and burn together (the default where your program supports it; `409 SIGNATURE_PAYMENT_UNAVAILABLE` where it doesn't). `"checkout"`: pay first through the returned `payment`, then burn.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="max_cost" type="number" required>
  The most the holder will pay for shipping, in dollars: pass the estimate's `total`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="cover_amount" type="number | null">
  Shipping insurance cover in dollars.
</ParamField>

## Response

**201** (200 with `"idempotent_replay": true` on a replay).

<ResponseField name="quote" type="object">
  The shipment: `quote_id`, `token_id`, `status`, `shipping_cost`, `holder`, `expires_at`, `paid_at`, `payment_refund_due`, `payment_method` (`signature` or `checkout`) and `burn` (null until paid). On a one-tap shipment `expires_at` is the end of the 30 minutes to sign.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payment" type="object | null">
  `method` says which. `"checkout"`: a Payment Intent, as for [Create a shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-shipment). `"signature"`: `amount` (the postage), `token` (the stablecoin) and `sign` (the typed data the holder signs). `null` on a replay once paid, or once a one-tap shipment's 30 minutes to sign have passed.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="idempotent_replay" type="boolean">Present and `true` on a replay.</ResponseField>

## Errors

The same as [Create a shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-shipment), except `BAD_TOKEN_IDS`, which doesn't apply. `TOKEN_IN_SHIPMENT` carries that shipment's `quote_id`. Also:

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `BAD_PAYMENT_METHOD` | `payment_method` isn't `signature` or `checkout` |
| `409` | `SIGNATURE_PAYMENT_UNAVAILABLE` | `payment_method: "signature"` sent, but one-tap shipping isn't set up for this token's contract; send `checkout` |
