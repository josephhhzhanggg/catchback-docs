---
title: "List sell-back quotes"
sidebarTitle: "List quotes"
api: "GET /api/partner/v2/quotes"
description: "Your program's sell-back quotes, newest first, paged."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

Newest first, each in the [Get a quote](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-quote) shape.

<ParamField query="wallet" type="string">
  The `holder` the quote was issued to, on your mint chain. Default: all.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="25">
  1-100.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  The `next_cursor` of the previous page.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="quotes" type="object[]">Each in the [Get a quote](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-quote) shape.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="next_cursor" type="string | null">Null on the last page.</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `BAD_CURSOR` | `cursor` isn't a `next_cursor` from a previous page |
| `400` | `BAD_LIMIT` | `limit` isn't 1-100 |
| `400` | `BAD_ADDRESS` | `wallet` is not an address on your mint chain |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v2/quotes?wallet=0x8f3c...e21a&limit=25" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
