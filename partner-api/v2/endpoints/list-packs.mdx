---
title: "List packs"
api: "GET /api/partner/v2/catalog/packs"
description: "The packs you can sell in one category, with price, odds choices, art and the chase on offer."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

The packs a wallet order can buy in one category, with your program's `mint_chain`.

Both catalog reads (List packs and Get a pack's odds and pool) can be up to a minute old: we cache them for a minute, so repeat reads are fast however often you call them (the first read after a quiet spell or a deploy can take several seconds). Stock is checked again when you create the order, so a pack that sold out in that minute is refused there: `409 ODDS_UNAVAILABLE` (with `odds`) on a pack that sells the odds choice, `409 PACK_UNAVAILABLE` (with its `reason`) on one that sells only its own odds.

<ParamField query="category" type="string" default="pokemon">
  `pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound`, `watches` or `goods`. `goods` packs hold streetwear and jewelry. Raw singles have no pack category of their own: they come out of other categories' packs, and an item pulled that way has `category: "singles"`.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="category" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="mint_chain" type="string">The chain your program mints on, e.g. `evm:196`. See [Chains](/partner-api/v2/chains-and-metadata).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="packs" type="object[]">
  <Expandable title="pack">
    <ResponseField name="pack_type" type="string">Pass it to [Get a pack's odds and pool](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-pack-pool) and [Create an order](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-order).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="price" type="number">What the customer pays, in dollars.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="available" type="boolean">Mirrors the pack's shelf in our app. A pack that isn't on sale right now stays listed with `available: false` and a `reason`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reason" type="string">Only when `available` is false: `Out of stock`, `Odds maintenance in progress`, `Restocking` or `Not available right now`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="odds" type="string[]">The [odds choices](/partner-api/v2/odds) the pack sells, in the order our app's picker shows them. Whether a particular choice is selling right now is on the pool endpoint.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="default_odds" type="string">The one an order gets when it names none (Standard wherever it is sold).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="image_url" type="string | null">The pack art: an absolute URL, resized for display, or null.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="banner_url" type="string | null">The pack's wide banner, or null.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="description" type="string | null">The pack's own text, or null.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="chase" type="object | null">
      The hand-picked chase item (bounty) the pack is offering right now, else null: `label`, `odds` (its chance per pack, formatted as a percentage), `ends_at` (null when open-ended) and `item` with its catalog fields and `market_value`.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
