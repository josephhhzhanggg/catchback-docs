---
title: "Get a quote"
api: "GET /api/partner/v2/quotes/{quoteId}"
description: "One sell-back quote and the progress of its USDC payout."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

A sell-back quote and its payout. We count a burn after a few block confirmations (EVM) or finalization (Solana), then pay within minutes. The amount paid is always exactly `amount_usd`. Every payout status change is also sent as a `sellback.updated` [webhook](/partner-api/v2/webhooks).

<ParamField path="quoteId" type="string" required>
  The `quote_id` from [Quote a sell-back](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-sellback-quote).
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="quote" type="object">
  <Expandable title="quote">
    <ResponseField name="quote_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`ready` (issued and not yet burned with), `expired` (not burned before `expires_at`; can no longer be used) or `redeemed` (we saw the burn; the payout is queued).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount_usd" type="number" />
    <ResponseField name="holder" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="payout" type="object">
      `chain`, `address`, `status`, `tx_hash` and `held`. `status`: `null` (no burn seen yet), `pending` (queued to send), `sending` (sent to the network, waiting to settle), `sent` (paid: `tx_hash` is the USDC transfer on `payout.chain`), `held` (under review by our team, `held: true`; see [Held payouts](/partner-api/v2/limits-and-compliance#held-payouts)) or `failed` (a send failed; it is retried automatically).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="expires_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="redeemed_at" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="burn_tx" type="string | null" />
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `404` | `UNKNOWN_QUOTE` | No such sell-back quote in your program |

```bash cURL
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
