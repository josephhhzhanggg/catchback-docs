---
title: "Get a customer's vault"
api: "GET /api/partner/v2/customers/{customer}/vault"
description: "Paying through you: the cards a customer holds now, each with what it sells back for at this moment."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

For [Paying through you](/partner-api/v2/settlement) programs only; a wallet program gets `400 NOT_PARTNER_SETTLED`.

The cards your orders pulled into this customer's vault that they still hold, newest first, each with what a sell-back pays right now. A sold or shipped card leaves the vault. A customer you have never sold to has an empty vault, not a `404`.

<ParamField path="customer" type="string" required>
  Your own id for the customer, as you sent it as `customer` on [Create an order](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-order). 1-128 characters of `A-Z a-z 0-9 _ . : -`.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="customer" type="string">The id you asked for.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="cards" type="object[]">
  Newest first.
  <Expandable title="card">
    <ResponseField name="card" type="object">`id` (pass it as `card_id` to [sell it back](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/sell-back-customer-card) or [ship it](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-customer-shipment)), `category` and `name`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="order_id" type="string">The `order_id` (your `Idempotency-Key`) of the order that pulled it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pulled_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="value_at_pull" type="number">The card's value when it was pulled, in dollars.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="sellback" type="object">
      What a sell-back pays right now: `amount` (dollars), `rate` (1 = 100%), `fresh` (true inside the fresh window after the pull, at the higher rate) and `locked_until` (for 7 days after a card is acquired its offer is based on its value when acquired, so a price change in that time doesn't move it).
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="truncated" type="boolean">`true` when the customer has more pulls than one read covers (200).</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `NOT_PARTNER_SETTLED` | Your program pays through customer wallets; read [a wallet's tokens](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/list-wallet-tokens) instead |
| `400` | `BAD_CUSTOMER` | `customer` isn't 1-128 characters of `A-Z a-z 0-9 _ . : -` |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v2/customers/user-1842/vault \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
