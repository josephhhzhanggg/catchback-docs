---
title: "Sell back a customer's card"
api: "POST /api/partner/v2/customers/{customer}/cards/{card_id}/sellback"
description: "Non-crypto model: sell one vault card back at its current offer, credited to your statement."
---

Scope: `nft:orders` · Rate limit: 30 a minute, shared with [Create an order](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-order) and [Create a customer shipment](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/create-customer-shipment)

For [Non-crypto model](/partner-api/v2/settlement) programs only; a crypto-model program gets `400 NOT_PARTNER_SETTLED` and sells back with a [quote and a burn](/partner-api/v2/sell-back).

Sells the card at its current offer (the `sellback.amount` [Get a customer's vault](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-customer-vault) shows) and credits the payout to your statement. Nothing is paid out to the customer: you credit them your way.

A card sells once: a retry returns the first sale with `status: "already_sold"` and never sells or credits again, so this endpoint needs no `Idempotency-Key`.

<ParamField path="customer" type="string" required>
  Your own id for the customer.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="card_id" type="string" required>
  A `card.id` from the customer's vault.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="min_payout" type="number">
  The least to accept, in dollars. A lower current offer is refused with `409 PRICE_CHANGED` and nothing is sold. Send what you showed your customer.
</ParamField>

## Response

**201** when sold; **200** with `status: "already_sold"` on a retry.

<ResponseField name="sellback" type="object">
  <Expandable title="sellback">
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`sold`, or `already_sold` (the first sale, unchanged).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="transaction_id" type="string">The sale. The [statement](/partner-api/v2/endpoints/get-settlement) entry's `sellback_id`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payout_amount" type="number">Credited to your statement, in dollars.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payout_rate" type="number | null">The rate paid (1 = 100%). Null on `already_sold`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="on_statement" type="boolean">Whether the credit is on your statement yet. Rarely `false`, and then it is added shortly.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `NOT_PARTNER_SETTLED` | Your program pays through customer wallets |
| `400` | `BAD_CUSTOMER` | `customer` isn't 1-128 characters of `A-Z a-z 0-9 _ . : -` |
| `400` | `BAD_CARD` | `card_id` isn't a card id |
| `400` | `BAD_JSON` | The body isn't JSON |
| `400` | `BAD_MIN_PAYOUT` | `min_payout` isn't a non-negative number |
| `404` | `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND` | No customer with that id has bought from your program |
| `404` | `CARD_NOT_IN_VAULT` | Your program didn't pull that card into this customer's vault |
| `409` | `PRICE_CHANGED` | The current offer is below `min_payout`; the body has `payout_amount` and `payout_rate`. Nothing is sold |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v2/customers/user-1842/cards/838a5e7f-dc62-4618-ad4a-cf015de20536/sellback \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "min_payout": 30 }'
```
</RequestExample>
