---
title: "Get your key"
api: "GET /api/partner/v2/keys"
description: "The key you call with: name, scopes, dates, program and payment mode, and its successor once rotated. Never a secret."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

The key you call with. Never a secret. Rotation and handling: [Authentication and keys](/partner-api/v2/authentication).

## Response

<ResponseField name="key" type="object">
  <Expandable title="key">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="scopes" type="string[]">`["nft:orders"]`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="last_used_at" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="expires_at" type="string | null">Set once the key has been rotated: when it stops working.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="rotated_from" type="string | null">The key this one replaced.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="successor" type="object | null">Once rotated, the key that replaced it: `id` and `name`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="program" type="string">Your program.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payment_mode" type="string">How your program is paid: `wallet` (customers pay from their own wallets and pulls mint as NFTs) or `partner` ([Non-crypto model](/partner-api/v2/settlement): send `customer` on orders and use the customer and statement endpoints).</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `401` | `INVALID_KEY` | The key is disabled, or a rotated key past its 24 hours |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v2/keys \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
