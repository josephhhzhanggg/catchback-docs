---
title: "Get a token"
api: "GET /api/partner/v2/tokens/{tokenId}"
description: "Any token of your program, live or burned: who holds it, the item behind it, and today's sell-back with its schedule."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

Any token of your program, live or burned: who holds it, the item behind it, and for a live token today's sell-back with its schedule.

<ParamField path="tokenId" type="string" required>
  The token's decimal `token_id`.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="token" type="object">
  <Expandable title="token">
    <ResponseField name="chain" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="contract" type="string">EVM. On a Solana program, `collection` and `asset` instead.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="token_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">The mint status (`pending`, `submitted`, `confirmed`, `failed`), or `burned`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="holder" type="string">The owner as our chain watcher last saw it (a quote re-reads the chain).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="order_id" type="string">The `Idempotency-Key` of the order that minted it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="minted_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="confirmed_at" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="end" type="object | null">
      A burned token has `end`: `{ "at": "...", "reason": "sellback" | "shipment" | "admin", "tx": "0x..." }`. `admin` means CatchBack burned it (a stuck or fraud case); contact us about it. Null for a live token.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="item" type="object">The item's catalog fields (name, set, year, number, grade, cert, brand, size, condition, images, whichever it has) and its current `market_value`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="sellback" type="object | null">
      Null for a burned token, or one not yet confirmed. Otherwise:
      <Expandable title="sellback">
        <ResponseField name="amount" type="number">What a sell-back pays right now, in dollars. Indicative: only a quote fixes it.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="rate" type="number">As a fraction, e.g. `0.8`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="fresh" type="boolean">True inside the 5-minute fresh window.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="value_locked" type="boolean">True while the 7-day value lock holds the amount.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="fresh_until" type="string">When the 5-minute fresh window ends (or ended).</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="locked_until" type="string">When the lock lifts.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="schedule" type="object[]">
          What a sell-back pays from now on, one phase per row: `from`, `until` (null for the last, open-ended phase), `rate` and `amount`. A `fixed: true` amount is locked; a `fixed: false` amount follows the market and is today's estimate.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `404` | `UNKNOWN_TOKEN` | No such token on your program's contract |

  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
