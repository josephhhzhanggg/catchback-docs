---
title: "Get your revenue statement"
api: "GET /api/partner/v2/revenue"
description: "Your revenue share, month by month: one month, or the last 12."
---

Scope: `nft:orders`

Your revenue share, month by month: one month with `period`, or the last 12 months (newest first) without it. A sale counts in the month it was paid; a refund in the month it was refunded. Amounts in USD.

**The standard share is 3% of GMV.** Premier partners earn a higher rate, set on their program. `share_pct` is the rate that applies to you.

<ParamField query="period" type="string">
  A month, `YYYY-MM`. Without it, the last 12 months.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="program" type="string">Your program.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="currency" type="string">`USD`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="months" type="object[]">
  <Expandable title="month">
    <ResponseField name="period" type="string">`YYYY-MM`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="orders" type="integer">Packs paid that month.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="gmv" type="number">Their price.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="share_pct" type="number">Your share rate.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="share_amount" type="number">What it earned on `gmv`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="refunds" type="object">Refunds that month: `count`, their price (`gmv`), and the share they take back (`share_amount`).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="net_gmv" type="number">After refunds.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="net_share" type="number">After refunds: what you are owed for the month.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payout" type="object">
      `status`: `open` (the month is still running), `due` (closed, owed and not paid yet), `paid` (with `amount`, `reference` and `paid_at`) or `none` (nothing owed).
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `BAD_PERIOD` | `period` isn't `YYYY-MM` |

  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
