Every card you pull or buy is held by CatchBack in your account's vault. [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault) lists it, with each card's sell-back offer right now. A pull can land in a different catalog from its pack's (a raw single from a Pokémon pack, for example), so read every category you might hold, `goods`, `singles` and `energy` included.

## What a sell-back pays

The same as any customer:

| The card was… | Within 5 minutes | For 7 days | After that |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pulled from a pack | 90% of the value at pull | 80% of the value at pull | 80% of market value |
| Bought | 80% of the lower of the price you paid and its market value at purchase | same | 80% of market value |

For the first 7 days the offer is fixed to the card's value when you got it, whether the market moves up or down. After that it follows the market. Because the 90% window is so short, decide on each pull as soon as the pack response arrives.

Some cards follow other rules:

- **Energy cards** from base packs pay 100% of their value, with no window and no lock.
- **Luxury goods** sell back only within 5 minutes of the pull.
- **A card from a pack that holds its cards** pays a flat 90% for the hold. It can't be kept, listed or shipped through the API, and we sell it back for you when the hold ends.
- **A card bought from another user** can carry over that seller's lock, if it's lower and still running ([Buy a card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card)).
- **Sealed products** aren't in your vault, and can't be sold back through the API.

The full rules: [Liquidity](/about-catchback/liquidity).

## Selling back

1. **Quote it, if you like.** [Quote a sell-back](/partner-api/endpoints/quote-sell-back) gives every amount a card will pay from now on, with the exact UTC time each one starts and ends. It moves no money and reserves nothing.
2. **Sell.** [Sell back cards](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back) takes 1–10 cards, each with its `pull_id` (for a pulled card) or `purchase_id` (for a bought one), from Get vault or the purchase response. To sell at a quoted price and nothing less, send that amount as `min_payout`: if the sell-back would now pay less, nothing is sold and you get the new amount.
3. **Read each result.** Each card comes back `sold`, `already_sold` or `failed`, and one failing doesn't stop the rest. A listed card fails until you [delist it](/partner-api/endpoints/delist-card).

Each pull or purchase pays out only once, so a retry is always safe: selling the same card again returns `already_sold` with the earlier sale. Sell-backs have no rate limit per key.
