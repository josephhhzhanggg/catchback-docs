---
title: "Get a withdrawal"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/withdrawals/{id}"
description: "One USDC withdrawal and its current status."
---

Scope: `withdraw:funds`, granted on request · REST only, no MCP tool

Poll this after [Withdraw](/partner-api/endpoints/create-withdrawal) until the status is `completed` or `failed`. A few seconds apart is plenty; most finish within minutes. With a webhook, [`withdrawal.updated`](/partner-api/webhook-events#withdrawal-updated) tells you instead.

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The withdrawal's `id`. A UUID.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="withdrawal" type="object">The fields of a withdrawal in [Withdraw](/partner-api/endpoints/create-withdrawal).</ResponseField>

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| `processing` | Sent, or being sent |
| `pending_review` | Held for our review, usually cleared within one business day |
| `completed` | Arrived; `tx_hashes` are on Base |
| `failed` | Didn't go through; the amount is back in your balance |

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | The id isn't a UUID |
| `403` | The key lacks `withdraw:funds` or isn't linked to an account |
| `404` | No withdrawal with that id on your account |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v1/withdrawals/3a6f1c2e-0000-4000-8000-000000000001 \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
