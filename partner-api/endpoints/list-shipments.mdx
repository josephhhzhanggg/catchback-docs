---
title: "List shipments"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/shipments"
description: "Your account's shipments, newest first, with their status and tracking. No addresses."
---

Scope: `ship:cards`, granted on request · MCP tool: `list_shipments`

Every shipment on your account, newest first, across every key linked to it. No address appears here: [Get a shipment](/partner-api/endpoints/get-shipment) has that.

<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">1-based.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="per_page" type="integer" default="50">1–100.</ParamField>

Unlike the other list endpoints, this one doesn't clamp: a `page` or `per_page` out of range is a `400`. See [Pagination](/partner-api/pagination).

## Response

<ResponseField name="page" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="per_page" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="total" type="integer | null">Shipments across all pages. `null` on a page past the end, which has no shipments.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="shipments" type="object[]">
  Newest first.
  <Expandable title="shipment">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">The shipment's id. [Get a shipment](/partner-api/endpoints/get-shipment) takes it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`pending`, `needs_review`, `processing`, `shipped`, `delivered` or `cancelled`: [what they mean](/partner-api/shipping#status).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="card_ids" type="string[]">The cards in the shipment.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="carrier" type="string | null">`null` until we've confirmed a rate.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="service" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="service_token" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="cost" type="number | null">What the shipment charges in USD. `null` until we've confirmed a rate.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="destination_country" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="tracking_number" type="string | null">`null` until there's a label.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="tracking_url" type="string | null">The carrier's tracking page. `null` until there's a label, or if we don't recognize the carrier.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="status_updated_at" type="string | null">When the status last changed. `null` until it first does. Between two [`shipment.updated`](/partner-api/webhook-events#shipment-updated) events, the later one is the newer.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="shipped_at" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="expected_delivery" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="delivered_at" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="needs_review" type="boolean">Present and `true` while `status` is `needs_review`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="address_removed" type="boolean">Present and `true` once we've removed the address: [how long we keep it](/partner-api/shipping#customer-addresses).</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | `page` isn't a positive number, or `per_page` isn't 1–100 |
| `403` | The key lacks `ship:cards` or isn't linked to an account |
| `500` | We couldn't load your shipments. Retry with backoff |

  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
