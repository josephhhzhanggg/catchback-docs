---
title: "Buy a card"
api: "POST /api/partner/v1/cards/purchase"
description: "Buy one card from the shop or a fixed-price user listing with your CatchCoins."
---

Scope: `buy:cards` · MCP tool: `buy_card` · **Moves money**

Charges the card's price plus a $1 buyer fee, from your cash CatchCoins balance only (no promo codes, bonus balance or card payments). The card lands in your [vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault), where you can hold it, [list it](/partner-api/endpoints/list-card) or [sell it back](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back). Find cards with [Search cards](/partner-api/endpoints/search-cards).

<ParamField header="Idempotency-Key" type="string" required>
  Your order id, 8–128 characters of `A-Za-z0-9_-`. Reuse it to retry the same order safely. See [Idempotency](/partner-api/errors-and-limits#idempotency-retrying-a-purchase-safely).
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="card_id" type="string" required>`card_id` from [Search cards](/partner-api/endpoints/search-cards).</ParamField>
<ParamField body="max_price" type="number" required>
  The most you'll pay for the card in dollars, before the fee. Usually the `price` the search showed. If the price is now higher, nothing is charged and you get `409 PRICE_CHANGED` with the new `price`.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="checkout_id" type="string">Our id for the order.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="transaction_id" type="string">The sale. It is the `purchase_id` that [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault) and [Sell back](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back) use for this card.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="source" type="string">`shop` or `listing`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="listing_id" type="string | null" />
<ResponseField name="price" type="number">Charged for the card.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="fee" type="number" />
<ResponseField name="total_charged" type="number">`price` + `fee`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="remaining_balance" type="number" />
<ResponseField name="item" type="object">A [card object](/partner-api/card-object).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="idempotent_replay" type="boolean">Present and `true` when this is a stored response to a retried order. Nothing was charged again.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="needs_review" type="boolean">
  Present and `true` when the sale went through but our bookkeeping needs a manual check. The card is yours; don't retry under a new order id. We're alerted automatically.
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | | Missing `Idempotency-Key`, a bad `card_id`, no `max_price`, or not enough balance |
| `404` | | No such card |
| `409` | `PRICE_CHANGED` | The price is above your `max_price`. The response has the current `price` |
| `409` | `CARD_NO_LONGER_AVAILABLE` | Sold, pulled from a pack, or delisted. Search again |
| `409` | `OWN_LISTING` | The card is your own listing |
| `409` | `IDEMPOTENCY_IN_PROGRESS` | An order with this key is still running |
| `422` | `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` | This key was used for a different order |
| `429` | `DAILY_CARD_LIMIT_REACHED` | Your daily card or spend cap. See [Card limits](/partner-api/errors-and-limits#card-limits) |

<Note>
  For 7 days after you buy a card, its sell-back offer is based on the lower of the price you paid and its market value when you bought it. See [Liquidity](/about-catchback/liquidity).
</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v1/cards/purchase \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-2077" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "card_id": "0e94dc61-…", "max_price": 1450 }'
```
</RequestExample>
