---
title: "Card activity"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/cards/{id}/activity"
description: "A card's recent sales and marketplace listings over the last 1–365 days, newest first, with nobody named."
---

Scope: `read:catalog` · MCP tool: `get_card_activity`

Two lists for one card: its sales, from the shop or a marketplace listing, and its listings. Nobody is named: there are no buyer or seller ids, usernames or listing text.

Only market trades count. A buy by one of CatchBack's own accounts (staff, test or content accounts) and a seller buying their own listing aren't market prices, so they are left out of `sales`, and the listing such a buy filled is left out of `listings`. Pack pulls and sell-backs to CatchBack aren't sales here.

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>A card id, as in [Get a card](/partner-api/endpoints/get-card).</ParamField>
<ParamField query="category" type="string">The card's catalog, if you know it. See [Get a card](/partner-api/endpoints/get-card).</ParamField>
<ParamField query="days" type="integer" default="90">How many days back from now: a whole number from 1 to 365. Anything else is a `400`.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="20">The most sales, and the most listings, returned: 1–50. Anything else is a `400`.</ParamField>

Activity is cached for up to a minute.

## Response

<ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="category" type="string">The card's catalog.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="days" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="limit" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="since" type="string">The start of the window: `days` before now.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="sales" type="object[]">
  Completed sales in the window, newest first.
  <Expandable title="sale">
    <ResponseField name="sale_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="sold_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="price" type="number">What the card sold for in USD, before the buyer fee.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="source" type="string">`shop` (CatchBack stock) or `listing` (a marketplace listing).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="listing_id" type="string | null">The listing it sold from. `null` for a shop sale.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="listing_type" type="string | null">`fixed_price` or `dutch_auction`. `null` for a shop sale.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="listings" type="object[]">
  Listings created or sold in the window, plus any listing still active whatever its age, newest first.
  <Expandable title="listing">
    <ResponseField name="listing_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="listing_type" type="string">`fixed_price` or `dutch_auction`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`active`, `sold`, `cancelled` or `expired`. New values can be added, so don't fail on one you don't know.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="price" type="number">The listing's price in USD. For a Dutch auction, the price stored on the listing rather than its current auction price: what it sold for is in `sales`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="listed_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="sold_at" type="string | null">`null` unless it sold.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | A malformed card id, an unknown `category`, `days` that isn't a whole number from 1 to 365, or `limit` that isn't one from 1 to 50 |
| `404` | No card has this id |
| `500` | We couldn't read the activity. Retry with backoff |
