---
title: "Estimate a shipment"
api: "POST /api/partner/v1/shipments/estimate"
description: "Live carrier rates for shipping cards you hold to a customer's address. Charges nothing and stores nothing."
---

Scope: `ship:cards`, granted on request · MCP tool: `estimate_shipment`

Live carrier rates, at the full rate you'd pay. Send `cover_amount` to price [insurance](/partner-api/shipping#insurance) as well. It charges nothing and stores nothing: the address only goes to our carrier partner for the quote. Take the `carrier` and `token` of the rate you choose to [Create a shipment](/partner-api/endpoints/create-shipment). See [Shipping cards](/partner-api/shipping) for how shipping works.

<ParamField body="card_ids" type="string[]" required>
  1–10 card ids from [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault), each once. A listed card has to be [delisted](/partner-api/endpoints/delist-card) first.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="address" type="object" required>
  Your customer's address.
  <Expandable title="address">
    <ParamField body="name" type="string" required>The recipient's name. At most 100 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="address1" type="string" required>At most 100 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="address2" type="string">At most 100 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="city" type="string" required>At most 100 characters.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="state" type="string">State or region, at most 100 characters. Required where the country has one and needs it, such as the US.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="zip" type="string">Postal code, at most 20 characters. Required, and checked against the country's format, where the country needs it, such as the US.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="country" type="string" required>ISO 3166 alpha-2, e.g. `US`. [Where you can ship](/partner-api/shipping#where-you-can-ship).</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="phone" type="string">At most 20 characters. Required outside the US.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="cover_amount" type="number">
  Optional. The dollars of insurance to price, with at most two decimals: from $100 up to the `max_cover` for these cards. It counts as two estimates. [How insurance works](/partner-api/shipping#insurance).
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="destination_country" type="string">The `country` you sent, in capitals.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="package_type" type="string">The parcel we'd use, e.g. `mailer`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="order_value" type="number">Our valuation of the cards, in USD.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="max_cover" type="number | null">The most you can insure: `order_value`, capped at \$10,000. `null` when the order is worth \$100 or less, which can't be insured.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="cover_amount" type="number">The amount you sent. Present only when you sent `cover_amount`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="rates" type="object[]">
  Cheapest first. Can be empty.
  <Expandable title="rate">
    <ResponseField name="carrier" type="string">e.g. `USPS`. Send it back as `rate.carrier`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="service" type="string">The service's name, e.g. `Priority Mail`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="token" type="string">e.g. `usps_priority`. Send it back as `rate.token`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="number">The full rate in USD, without any premium. Normally what you send as `max_cost`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="estimated_days" type="number | null">The carrier's estimate, in days.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="cover_available" type="boolean">Present when you sent `cover_amount`. `false` when no premium is quoted for this service.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="insurance_premium" type="number | null">Present when you sent `cover_amount`. `null` when `cover_available` is `false`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total" type="number | null">Present when you sent `cover_amount`: `amount` plus `insurance_premium`, which is what you send as `max_cost` to insure this rate. `null` when `cover_available` is `false`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | | The body isn't JSON, `card_ids` isn't 1–10 card ids (or lists one twice), the address is invalid, or `cover_amount` isn't a dollar amount with at most two decimals or is 0 or negative. An address error is `"error": "Invalid address"` with `fields`, which names each bad field and never repeats its value |
| `400` | `COVER_NOT_ALLOWED` | `cover_amount` is outside the range: from \$100 up to the cards' value, capped at \$10,000. The body has `order_value`, `min_cover` and `max_cover` |
| `400` | `COUNTRY_NOT_SUPPORTED` | We don't ship to this country. A country code we don't recognize is a plain `400` (`"Invalid address"`, with `fields.country`) |
| `400` | `OVER_CUSTOMS_CAP` | An international shipment worth over $2,500, or with a card we have no value for |
| `400` | `PARTNER_GOODS` | A partner's own goods ship from that partner |
| `403` | | The key lacks `ship:cards` or isn't linked to an account, the account needs a verified phone number (`"Phone verification required"`), the account is deleted (`"Partner account unavailable"`), or it can't request shipping |
| `403` | `FEATURE_RESTRICTED` | The account is restricted from the marketplace or from withdrawals (`capability` is `marketplace` or `withdraw`). Contact us |
| `409` | `CARDS_NOT_SHIPPABLE` | A card can't ship. `cards[]` gives each refused `card_id` and its `reason`: [what they mean](/partner-api/shipping#which-cards-can-ship) |
| `429` | `RATE_LIMITED` | More than 60 estimates a minute on this key, where one with `cover_amount` counts as two. Wait `retry_after_seconds`, also sent as a `Retry-After` header |
| `502` | | The carrier quote failed. Try again |
| `503` | | We couldn't check the account or the cards. Try again |
