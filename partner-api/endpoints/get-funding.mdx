---
title: "Get funding details"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/funding"
description: "Your USDC deposit addresses, and where your withdrawals go."
---

Scope: `read:account` · REST only, no MCP tool

The addresses are yours permanently and never change. A deposit to one lands in your balance once it settles. See [Funding in USDC](/partner-api/funding).

## Response

<ResponseField name="deposit" type="object">
  <Expandable title="deposit">
    <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">`USDC`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="credited_to" type="string">`balance`: a deposit adds to your CatchCoins balance.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="addresses" type="object[]">
      One per kind of chain, each `{ chain_type, address }`. `ethereum` takes USDC on the EVM chains we accept, Base included; `solana` takes Solana USDC.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="withdrawal" type="object | null">
  Where withdrawals go. `null` until we've set your withdrawal address.
  <Expandable title="withdrawal">
    <ResponseField name="network" type="string">`USDC on Base`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="address" type="string">Your escrow address.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="min_amount" type="number">The smallest withdrawal, in USD.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="daily_limit" type="number">Withdrawals past this over 24 hours are held for review.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fee" type="number">`0`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `403` | The key lacks `read:account` or isn't linked to an account |
| `502` | We couldn't load the addresses just now. Retry shortly |
| `503` | USDC funding is switched off |

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v1/funding \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
