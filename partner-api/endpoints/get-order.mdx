---
title: "Get an order"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/orders/{checkout_id}"
description: "One order by its id, including one that is still running, with every pull or card in it."
---

Scope: `read:account` · MCP tool: `get_order`

One order on your account, with the same fields as in [List orders](/partner-api/endpoints/list-orders) plus what it contains: a pack purchase's pulls, or the card for any other order.

It answers while an order is still running. After a timeout, or a `409 IDEMPOTENCY_IN_PROGRESS` that carries the order's `checkout_id`, follow the order here rather than resending it in a tight loop: it shows `pending` until the order completes or we close it, and a pack order's pulls as soon as they have landed. See [Idempotency](/partner-api/idempotency).

<ParamField path="checkout_id" type="string" required>
  The `checkout_id` that [Buy packs](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-packs) or [Buy a card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card) returned, the `transaction_id` of a [sell-back](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back), or any order's `checkout_id` from List orders. A UUID.
</ParamField>

## Response

Every field of an order in [List orders](/partner-api/endpoints/list-orders), plus:

<ResponseField name="pulls" type="object[]">
  A pack purchase's pulls, in order. Empty for any other order.
  <Expandable title="pull">
    <ResponseField name="pull_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="kind" type="string | null">`card`, or `sealed_pack` for a sealed product (a pull from the Starter or a sealed mystery pack, or a consolation pull). `null` if we can't find the pull.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="item" type="object | null">The card as it is now: a [card object](/partner-api/card-object), with today's images and market value. A sealed pack is `{ id, name, set, category, image_url }`. `null` if we can't load it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="value" type="number | null">Market value at the moment of the pull.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="hit" type="object | null">`rarity_tier`, `bucket`, `bucket_odds`, `is_chase` and `chase_label`, as the purchase returned them. `null` while the order is still running, and for an order we closed after its response was lost.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="item" type="object | null">The card, for a card buy, a sell-back or a sale: a [card object](/partner-api/card-object) as it is now. `null` for a pack purchase, or if we can't load the card.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payout_rate" type="number | null">Sell-backs only: the rate the sell-back paid at.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="basis_value" type="number | null">Sell-backs only: the value the payout was based on.</ResponseField>

A pull here has no `catchback` offer and no `proof`. For a card's offer right now, read [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault). To check a pull, see [Verify a pull](/partner-api/verify-a-pull).

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `403` | The key lacks `read:account` or isn't linked to an account |
| `404` | No order with this id on your account. An unknown id, a malformed one and another account's order all look the same |
| `500` | We couldn't load the order. Retry with backoff |
