---
title: "Get a pack's pool"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/catalog/packs/pool"
description: "Everything a pack can pull right now: its value bands, their odds, and every hittable card or sealed product."
---

Scope: `read:catalog` · MCP tool: `get_pack_pool`

A pack is a set of **value bands** (e.g. \$5–\$20, \$20–\$50). Opening a pack picks a band by its odds, then a card from that band. This endpoint returns the bands, their odds at the risk level you ask for, the chase card if one is set, and every card a pull can produce right now. It is the same data behind the odds panel and "possible hits" on our pack pages.

A band at 0% odds (out of stock, or zeroed by the risk level) is still listed, but its cards are not, since a pull can't produce them. The pool is [point-in-time](/partner-api/errors-and-limits#inventory-is-point-in-time): any opening on CatchBack can take a card from it.

## Slab packs and sealed packs

Most packs pay out graded cards (slabs). The Starter pack and the sealed mystery packs pay out **sealed products** instead (loose booster packs, bundles and the like). `kind` tells you which you have:

- **`kind: "slab"`**: the bands are value ranges, and `cards` lists every hittable card.
- **`kind: "sealed"`**: every in-stock product is its own band, `products` lists them with their odds, and `cards` is empty. The odds are the ones the real draw uses: when a product sells out, its share is spread evenly over what is still in stock. `chase` is always `null`.

A sold-out pack still returns `200` with `available: false`, not an error; its pool may be empty.

<ParamField query="pack_type" type="string" required>
  A `pack_type` from [List packs](/partner-api/endpoints/list-packs). A pack you can't buy is a `404`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="category" type="string" default="pokemon">
  `pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound` or `watches`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="risk_level" type="string" default="standard">
  `chill`, `standard` or `aggressive` (Hot in the app). Odds change with the risk level, and so can which cards are hittable. Use one of the pack's `risk_levels` from [List packs](/partner-api/endpoints/list-packs); any other is a `400`. The Starter pack opens at `standard` only.
</ParamField>

    <ResponseField name="bucket_id" type="string">Band id. Each card's `bucket_id` points here.</ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="min_value" type="number" />
    <ResponseField name="max_value" type="number" />
    <ResponseField name="odds" type="number">Probability of landing in this band, 0–1.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="is_consolation" type="boolean">A consolation outcome (a sealed pack rather than a card).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="hittable_cards" type="integer">How many cards in `cards` belong to this band. Always `0` for a sealed pack.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="probability" type="number" />
    <ResponseField name="odds_label" type="string">e.g. `1 in 5,000`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="label" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="ends_at" type="string | null" />
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="cards" type="object[]">

<ResponseField name="total_products" type="integer">Sealed packs only. How many products are in `products`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="products" type="object[]">
  Sealed packs only. Every in-stock product, highest value first.
  <Expandable title="product">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="bucket_id" type="string">This product's band in `bands`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="set" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="product_type" type="string | null">e.g. `loose_pack`, `booster_bundle`.</ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="year" type="number | null" />
    <ResponseField name="image" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="market_value" type="number">USD.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="in_stock" type="integer">Units we hold right now.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="odds" type="number">Probability of pulling this product, 0–1.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
