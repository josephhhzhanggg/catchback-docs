title: "Quote a sell-back"
api: "POST /api/partner/v1/cards/sell-back/quote"
description: "What a sell-back pays for each card you hold, now and at every point after, with the exact UTC time each amount starts and ends."
---

Scope: `sell:cards` · MCP tool: `quote_sell_back`

A quote moves no money and reserves nothing. It tells you what [Sell back cards](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back) would pay for each card right now, and when that changes: for a fresh pull, 90% until 5 minutes after the pull, then 80% of the value at pull until 7 days after it, then 80% of market value. Each phase comes with the exact UTC instant it starts and ends.

A quote holds while the card stays in your vault. The last phase follows the market, so its amount is today's estimate. To sell at a quoted price and nothing less, send its `amount` as `min_payout` on [Sell back cards](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back).

<ParamField body="category" type="string" default="pokemon">
  The catalog the cards are in: `pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound`, `watches`, `goods`, `singles` or `energy` (the energy cards from base packs). One category per request, as in [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="items" type="object[]" required>
  1–100 cards, each `{ card_id }`. An item may also name the `pull_id` or `purchase_id` you'll sell it back by (at most one); it fails if that's no longer the card's latest acquisition. The same card can't be listed twice.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="category" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="quoted_at" type="string">When the quote was made, in UTC.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="results" type="object[]">
  One per item, in the order you sent them.
  <Expandable title="result">
    <ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="pull_id" type="string | null">The card's latest acquisition, when it's a pull. Sell back by this.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="purchase_id" type="string | null">The card's latest acquisition, when it's a buy.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`quoted`, `not_offered` (with `reason`) or `failed` (with `error`, e.g. a card you don't hold).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reason" type="string">Only on `not_offered`: `listed` ([delist it](/partner-api/endpoints/delist-card) first), or `fresh_window_closed` for a luxury goods item more than 5 minutes after its pull.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="catchback" type="object">
      Only on `quoted`.
      <Expandable title="catchback">
        <ResponseField name="amount" type="number">What a sell-back pays right now, in USD.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="rate" type="number" />
        <ResponseField name="valid_until" type="string | null">When the current amount ends. `null` when it doesn't.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="schedule" type="object[]">
          Every phase from now on, back to back. A sell-back sent between `valid_from` and `valid_until` pays `amount`.
          <Expandable title="phase">
            <ResponseField name="amount" type="number" />
            <ResponseField name="rate" type="number" />
            <ResponseField name="basis" type="string">`value_at_acquisition` (the value when you got the card) or `market_value`.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="basis_value" type="number">The value the rate applies to.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="fixed" type="boolean">`true`: the amount is locked and won't change. `false`: it follows the market, and `amount` is today's estimate.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="valid_from" type="string" />
            <ResponseField name="valid_until" type="string | null">`null` on the last phase.</ResponseField>
          </Expandable>
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="auto_sold_at" type="string | null">For a card from a pack that holds its cards, when we sell it back for you.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Special cases

- **Energy cards** (`category: "energy"`) have one phase: 100% of their value, with no 5-minute window and no lock.
- **A card from a pack that holds its cards** has one 90% phase, ending at `auto_sold_at`, when it's sold back automatically.
- **A luxury goods item** has only its 5-minute phase; after that it's `not_offered`.
- **Insurance or a boost** bought on the pull raises the first phase's amount, inside the 5-minute window.

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | The body isn't JSON, the category is unknown, `items` is missing, empty or longer than 100, an item lacks a UUID `card_id` or names both a `pull_id` and a `purchase_id`, or the same card is listed twice |
| `403` | The key lacks `sell:cards` or isn't linked to an account |
| `500` | We couldn't read the cards or their offers. Nothing changed; retry |
