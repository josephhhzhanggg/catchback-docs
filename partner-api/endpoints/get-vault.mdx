---
title: "Get vault"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/vault"
description: "The cards you hold, each with the pull it came from and its CatchBack offer right now."
---

Scope: `read:account` · MCP tool: `get_vault`

Cards in your vault or listed on the marketplace. Cards that have shipped are not included.

<ParamField query="category" type="string" default="pokemon">
  `pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound` or `watches`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">1-based.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="per_page" type="integer" default="100">1–200.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="category" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="page" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="per_page" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="total" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="cards" type="object[]">
  <Expandable title="held card">
    <ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="pull_id" type="string | null">The pull the card came from. [Sell back](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back) needs it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pulled_at" type="string | null" />
    <ResponseField name="item" type="object">A [card object](/partner-api/card-object).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="value" type="number">Current market value.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="listed" type="boolean">Listed on the marketplace.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="catchback" type="object | null">
      The sell-back offer right now: `amount`, `rate`, `basis_value` (the value the offer is based on) and `locked_value_until`. `null` while the card is listed. Delist it first.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Note>
  For 7 days after you acquire a card, its offer is based on its value when you acquired it, fixed whether the market moves up or down (`locked_value_until`). After that it follows the current market value. See [Liquidity](/about-catchback/liquidity).
</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v1/vault?category=pokemon&page=1&per_page=100" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
