---
title: "Sell back cards"
api: "POST /api/partner/v1/cards/sell-back"
description: "Sell cards you pulled or bought back to CatchBack for CatchCoins, at the same rates as every customer."
---

Scope: `sell:cards` · MCP tool: `sell_back_cards`

Pays the same rate a customer gets. A pulled card: the pull's fresh rate (90%) within 5 minutes of the pull, then 80% of the value at pull for 7 days, then 80% of market value. A [bought card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card): 80% of the lower of the price you paid and its market value at purchase for 7 days, then 80% of market value. See [Liquidity](/about-catchback/liquidity).

Items run in order, and one failing doesn't stop the rest. A pull or purchase pays out only once: selling it again returns `already_sold` with the earlier sale, so a retry is always safe. Cards only; sealed packs from consolation pulls can't be sold back through the API yet. Sell-back is not rate-limited.

<ParamField body="items" type="object[]" required>
  1–10 cards, each `{ card_id, pull_id }` for a pulled card or `{ card_id, purchase_id }` for a bought one (exactly one of the two). Take them from [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault), from the pulls in [Buy packs](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-packs) (item `id` and `pull_id`), or from [Buy a card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card) (`card_id` and `transaction_id` as `purchase_id`). A listed card must be delisted first.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="total_paid" type="number">USD credited across all items.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="remaining_balance" type="number" />
<ResponseField name="results" type="object[]">
  <Expandable title="result">
    <ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="pull_id" type="string">Echoed when you sent one.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="purchase_id" type="string">Echoed when you sent one.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`sold`, `already_sold` (paid earlier; the earlier sale is returned), or `failed` (with `error`).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="transaction_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="payout_amount" type="number" />
    <ResponseField name="payout_rate" type="number" />
    <ResponseField name="error" type="string">Why it failed, when `status` is `failed`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v1/cards/sell-back \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "items": [{ "card_id": "bee48cce-…", "pull_id": "873deb80-…" }] }'
```
</RequestExample>
