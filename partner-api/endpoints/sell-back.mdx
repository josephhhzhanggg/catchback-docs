---
title: "Sell back cards"
api: "POST /api/partner/v1/cards/sell-back"
description: "Sell cards you pulled or bought back to CatchBack, paid to your Wallet Balance, at the same rates as every customer."
---

Scope: `sell:cards` · MCP tool: `sell_back_cards`

Pays the same rate a customer gets. A pulled card: the pull's fresh rate within 5 minutes of the pull (100% for a pack opened at `standard`, 90% at `chill` or `aggressive`; the pull's `catchback.rate` says which), then 80% of the value at pull for 7 days, then 80% of market value. A [bought card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card): 80% of the lower of the price you paid and its market value at purchase for 7 days, then 80% of market value. An energy card from a base pack pays 100% of its value. See [Liquidity](/about-catchback/liquidity), and [Quote a sell-back](/partner-api/endpoints/quote-sell-back) for every amount ahead of time.

Items run in order, and one failing doesn't stop the rest. A pull or purchase pays out only once: selling it again returns `already_sold` with the earlier sale, so a retry is always safe. Cards only: a sealed pack (any `sealed_pack` pull, from the Starter, a sealed mystery pack or a consolation pull) can't be sold back through the API. A luxury goods item (category `goods`) sells back only within 5 minutes of its pull. Sell-backs have no rate limit per key.

<ParamField body="items" type="object[]" required>
  1–10 cards, each `{ card_id, pull_id }` for a pulled card or `{ card_id, purchase_id }` for a bought one (exactly one of the two). Take them from [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault), from the pulls in [Buy packs](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-packs) (item `id` and `pull_id`), or from [Buy a card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card) (`card_id` and `transaction_id` as `purchase_id`). A listed card must be delisted first.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="items[].min_payout" type="number">
  The least you'll accept for this card, in USD: usually the `amount` from [Quote a sell-back](/partner-api/endpoints/quote-sell-back). If the sell-back would pay less (say the 5-minute window closed first), nothing is sold and the item fails with `PRICE_CHANGED`, `payout_amount` and `payout_rate` set to what it pays now. Send it again with that amount if you accept it.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="total_paid" type="number">USD credited by this request: the `sold` items only. An `already_sold` item was paid earlier and isn't counted again.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="remaining_balance" type="number | null">Your Wallet Balance after the request, or `null` if we couldn't read it.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="results" type="object[]">
  One per item, in the order you sent them.
  <Expandable title="result">
    <ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="pull_id" type="string">Echoed when you sent one.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="purchase_id" type="string">Echoed when you sent one.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`sold`, `already_sold` (paid earlier; the earlier sale is returned), or `failed` (with `error`).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="transaction_id" type="string">The sell-back. For `already_sold`, the earlier one. Not on `failed`. [Get an order](/partner-api/endpoints/get-order) takes it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payout_amount" type="number">What the sell-back paid. For `already_sold`, what the earlier one paid. For `PRICE_CHANGED`, what it would pay now. Not on other `failed` items.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payout_rate" type="number">The rate it paid at. Only on `sold`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="error" type="string">Why it failed. Only on `failed`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="code" type="string">A machine-readable reason, on some `failed` items. See the table below.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

The whole request is refused when:

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | The body isn't JSON, `items` is missing, empty or longer than 10, an item lacks a UUID `card_id` or doesn't have exactly one of `pull_id` and `purchase_id`, a `min_payout` isn't a non-negative number, or the same pull or purchase of a card is listed twice |
| `403` | The key lacks `sell:cards` or isn't linked to an account, or the account is unavailable |

Otherwise the response is a `200`, and each item that couldn't be sold comes back with `status: "failed"`, an `error`, and sometimes a `code`:

| Code | When |
|---|---|
| `CARD_LISTED_ON_MARKETPLACE` | The card is listed. [Delist it](/partner-api/endpoints/delist-card) first |
| `GOODS_SELLBACK_WINDOW_CLOSED` | A luxury goods item, more than 5 minutes after its pull |
| `CARD_IN_BATTLE` | The card is held by a pack battle that hasn't finished |
| `SHOP_PURCHASE_NOT_SELLABLE` | An item bought from a partner shop, which can ship but doesn't sell back |
| `PRICE_CHANGED` | It would pay less than your `min_payout`. Nothing was sold; `payout_amount` is what it pays now |

A `failed` item without a `code` explains itself in `error`, e.g. a `purchase_id` that isn't a purchase of this card by your account, or a card that has shipped. Read `error` for anything you don't branch on.

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v1/cards/sell-back \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "items": [{ "card_id": "bee48cce-3d1f-4c2a-8b7e-5a9d0c4f1e62", "pull_id": "873deb80-6c2b-4e1d-9f35-2a7b8c0d4e91" }] }'
```
</RequestExample>
