---
title: "Purchase report"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/pulls"
description: "Completed pack purchases in a time window, for revenue-share reporting and attribution."
---

Scope: `read:pulls` · REST only, not an MCP tool

Every completed pack purchase in a window, priced the same way our own revenue reporting is, so your totals reconcile with ours. Purchases with an `amount` of 0 (comped packs) and our own internal accounts are excluded.

It returns nothing about what was pulled and nothing about the buyer beyond an opaque id: no cards, no email, username, phone, address or balance. That is permanent for this scope.

<ParamField query="since" type="string">ISO 8601, inclusive. Defaults to `until` minus 7 days.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="until" type="string">ISO 8601, inclusive. Defaults to now.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">1-based.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="per_page" type="integer" default="500">1–1000.</ParamField>

The window is capped at **31 days**, and a malformed or inverted window is a `400`. There is no "all of history" mode, so pull a long report a month at a time.

<Tip>
  For a monthly report, pin both `since` and `until` in the past. The `until = now` default moves between your page requests. Purchases are only ever added, so a closed window pages consistently.
</Tip>

<ResponseField name="until" type="string" />

<ResponseField name="pulls" type="object[]">
  <Expandable title="purchase">
    <ResponseField name="user_id" type="string">The buyer's opaque, stable CatchBack id. Join on it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string">When the pack was bought (UTC).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="number">Sale price in USD.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="source" type="string">The catalog: `pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound`, `custom`, `loose` or `steph`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pack_type" type="string">The tier or named pack.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

```bash cURL
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
