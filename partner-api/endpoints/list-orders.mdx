---
title: "List orders"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/orders"
description: "Your account's order history, newest first: pack and card purchases, sell-backs and sales of your listings."
---

Scope: `read:account` · MCP tool: `list_orders`

Everything that moved your balance through the API, newest first, so you can reconcile your records against ours. There are four kinds of order:

| `type` | What it is | Its `checkout_id` |
|---|---|---|
| `pack_purchase` | A [Buy packs](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-packs) order | The `checkout_id` the purchase returned |
| `card_purchase` | A [Buy a card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card) order | The `checkout_id` the purchase returned |
| `sell_back` | One card [sold back](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back). A request that sells several cards makes one order per card | The sell-back's `transaction_id` |
| `listing_sale` | A sale of one of your listings to someone else, fixed price or Dutch auction | The sale's id |

The history belongs to the account, not the key: every key linked to the account sees the same orders. Purchases and sell-backs made in our app are left out. A sale of one of your listings is in, wherever you listed the card.

A key acts as one CatchBack account, so the history can't say which of your own customers an order was for. Keep that on your side, against the `order_id` you chose or the order's `pull_ids`.

<ParamField query="type" type="string">
  Only orders of this kind: `pack_purchase`, `card_purchase`, `sell_back` or `listing_sale`. Leave it out for all four.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="since" type="string">ISO 8601 with a time zone, e.g. `2026-10-01T00:00:00Z`. Inclusive. Defaults to `until` minus 7 days.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="until" type="string">ISO 8601 with a time zone. Inclusive. Defaults to now. At most 31 days after `since`.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="50">Orders per page, 1–100. A higher value is treated as 100.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  `next_cursor` from the previous page, unchanged. It carries that page's window and type, so `since`, `until` and `type` are ignored when you send it. See [Pagination](/partner-api/pagination#cursor-pages).
</ParamField>

The window is capped at **31 days**, and a malformed or inverted window is a `400`, as on the [Purchase report](/partner-api/endpoints/purchase-report). To read a longer stretch, ask for it a month at a time.

## Response

<ResponseField name="since" type="string">The window's start, as used.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="until" type="string">The window's end, as used.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="type" type="string | null">The `type` filter, or `null` for all four.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="limit" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="has_more" type="boolean">Whether there are more orders in the window.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="next_cursor" type="string | null">Send it as `cursor` for the next page. `null` on the last page.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="orders" type="object[]">
  Newest first, by `created_at`.
  <Expandable title="order">
    <ResponseField name="checkout_id" type="string">The order's id: see the table above. [Get an order](/partner-api/endpoints/get-order) takes it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="order_id" type="string | null">The `Idempotency-Key` you sent with a purchase. `null` for a sell-back or a sale.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="string">`pack_purchase`, `card_purchase`, `sell_back` or `listing_sale`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">
      `completed`, or `pending` while a purchase is still running, until we close an interrupted one ([how](/partner-api/idempotency#timeouts-and-interrupted-orders)), or while one is held for review ([why](/partner-api/errors-and-limits#when-a-purchase-needs-review)). A purchase that failed and refunded itself isn't listed, and neither is a held one once we've settled it. New values can be added, so don't fail on one you don't know.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="completed_at" type="string | null">`null` while the order is pending.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="number | null">What a purchase charged (a card buy includes its $1 buyer fee), what a sell-back paid, or what a listing sold for, in USD.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fee" type="number | null">The $1 buyer fee on a card buy or the $1 seller fee on a sale. `0` on a pack purchase or a sell-back.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="balance_change" type="number | null">
      The order's effect on your balance: −`amount` for a purchase, +`amount` for a sell-back, +(`amount` − `fee`) for a sale. `null` until something shows the order was charged. A pending purchase whose packs or card have already landed shows its charge here before we close it.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reconciled" type="boolean">`true` when the order went through but its response was lost, and we closed it from our own records.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pack_type" type="string | null">Pack purchases only.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="quantity" type="integer | null">Pack purchases only.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pull_ids" type="string[]">A pack purchase's pulls. For a sell-back of a card you pulled, the pull it sold. Empty otherwise.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="card_id" type="string | null">The card a card buy, a sell-back or a sale was for. `null` on a pack purchase.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="listing_id" type="string | null">The listing a card buy or a sale came from. `null` for a shop buy, a pack purchase or a sell-back.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="transaction_id" type="string | null">
      For a card buy, the sale: the `purchase_id` that [Get vault](/partner-api/endpoints/get-vault) and [Sell back](/partner-api/endpoints/sell-back) use. For a sell-back or a sale, the same id as `checkout_id`. `null` on a pack purchase.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | An unknown `type`, a `since` or `until` we can't read as a date, `since` not before `until`, a window wider than 31 days, or a `cursor` that wasn't passed back unchanged |
| `403` | The key lacks `read:account` or isn't linked to an account |
| `500` | We couldn't read your history. Retry with backoff: you never get a partial page |
