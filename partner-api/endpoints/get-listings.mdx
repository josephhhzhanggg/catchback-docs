---
title: "Your listings"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/listings"
description: "Your account's active marketplace listings, newest first, with what each one pays you if it sells."
---

Scope: `read:account` · MCP tool: `get_listings`

[Search cards](/partner-api/endpoints/search-cards) leaves your own listings out. This lists them: each `listing_id`, the card, its price, and your payout if it sells, which is the price less the $1 seller fee. Use the `listing_id` to [change a listing's price](/partner-api/endpoints/update-listing-price). A Dutch auction you started in our app shows here too, at its current auction price.

<ParamField query="category" type="string">
  Only this catalog: `pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound` or `watches`. Leave it out for every catalog.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">1-based. See [Pagination](/partner-api/pagination).</ParamField>
<ParamField query="per_page" type="integer" default="100">1–200. A higher value is treated as 200.</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="page" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="per_page" type="integer" />
<ResponseField name="total" type="integer | null">Active listings across all pages.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="has_more" type="boolean">Whether another page has listings.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="listings" type="object[]">
  Newest first.
  <Expandable title="listing">
    <ResponseField name="listing_id" type="string">Send it to [Change a listing's price](/partner-api/endpoints/update-listing-price).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="card_id" type="string" />
    <ResponseField name="category" type="string | null">The card's catalog. `null` on some older listings, which [Change a listing's price](/partner-api/endpoints/update-listing-price) answers with `409 LISTING_NOT_ACTIVE`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="listing_type" type="string">`fixed_price`, or `dutch_auction` for a Dutch auction started in our app.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="price" type="number">The listing's price right now, in USD: what a buyer is charged for the card, before the $1 buyer fee. For a Dutch auction, its current auction price.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="seller_fee" type="number">What a sale takes from your payout: $1.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payout" type="number">What your balance is credited if it sells at `price`: `price` − `seller_fee`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="listed_at" type="string">When you listed it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="updated_at" type="string | null">When the listing last changed, a price change included.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="item" type="object | null">A [card object](/partner-api/card-object), or `null` if we can't load the card.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | An unknown `category` (the five above) |
| `403` | The key lacks `read:account` or isn't linked to an account |
| `500` | We couldn't load your listings. Retry with backoff |
