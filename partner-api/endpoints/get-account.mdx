---
title: "Get account"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/account"
description: "Your balance, and where you stand against your pack and card limits."
---

Scope: `read:account` · MCP tool: `get_balance`

Enough to plan your next pack or card purchase without hitting a `429`. See [Purchase limits](/partner-api/errors-and-limits#purchase-limits).

## Response

<ResponseField name="username" type="string" />
<ResponseField name="balance" type="number">CatchCoins balance in USD.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="limits" type="object">
  <Expandable title="limits">
    <ResponseField name="packs_today" type="integer">Packs opened this UTC day.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="max_packs_per_day" type="integer" />
    <ResponseField name="packs_left_today" type="integer" />
    <ResponseField name="resets_at" type="string">Next midnight UTC.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="min_seconds_between_purchases" type="integer" />
    <ResponseField name="next_purchase_at" type="string">The earliest your next pack purchase is allowed.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="card_limits" type="object">
  Your daily caps on [card buys](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card). An order still running counts at its `max_price` + $1, the same way the cap counts it. The usage fields are `null` if we couldn't read them.
  <Expandable title="card_limits">
    <ResponseField name="cards_today" type="integer | null">Cards bought this UTC day.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="max_cards_per_day" type="integer" />
    <ResponseField name="cards_left_today" type="integer | null" />
    <ResponseField name="spend_today" type="number | null">USD spent on cards this UTC day, fees included.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="max_card_spend_per_day" type="number" />
    <ResponseField name="spend_left_today" type="number | null" />
    <ResponseField name="resets_at" type="string">Next midnight UTC.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://catchbackcards.com/api/partner/v1/account \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CATCHBACK_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
