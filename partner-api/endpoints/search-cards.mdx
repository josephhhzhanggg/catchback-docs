---
title: "Search cards"
api: "GET /api/partner/v1/marketplace/listings"
description: "Every card you can buy right now: shop stock and fixed-price user listings, filtered by name, grade and price."
---

Scope: `read:catalog` · MCP tool: `search_cards`

Each result is buyable with [Buy a card](/partner-api/endpoints/buy-card) unless someone else takes it first. `price` is exactly what the buy charges for the card, and `total_cost` adds the $1 buyer fee. Dutch auctions and your own listings are left out. Results are [point-in-time](/partner-api/errors-and-limits#inventory-is-point-in-time).

<ParamField query="category" type="string" default="pokemon">
  `pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound` or `watches`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="q" type="string">
  Text in the card name, slab label or set, e.g. `charizard`. 2–60 characters, each a letter, a digit, a space, or one of `'` `&` `.` `-`. Anything else is a `400`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="grading_company" type="string">
  2–10 letters, e.g. `PSA`, `BGS`, `CGC`. Matched without regard to case.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="min_grade" type="number" />
<ParamField query="max_grade" type="number" />
<ParamField query="min_price" type="number">Dollars.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="max_price" type="number">Dollars.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="source" type="string" default="all">`all`, `shop` (CatchBack stock) or `listing` (another user's fixed-price listing).</ParamField>
<ParamField query="sort" type="string" default="price_asc">`price_asc` or `price_desc`. Equal prices are ordered by `card_id`.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">1–20. A higher page is treated as 20. See [Pagination](/partner-api/pagination).</ParamField>
<ParamField query="per_page" type="integer" default="50">1–100. A higher value is treated as 100.</ParamField>

<Note>
  The `q`, `grading_company` and grade filters are applied to user listings after we take the first 500 listings in the category by price (cheapest first, or priciest first with `price_desc`). In a category with more listings than that, a filtered search can miss matches past the first 500, and `has_more` stays `true`. `min_price` and `max_price` apply before that cut, so a price band reaches the rest. Shop stock is filtered in full.
</Note>

<ResponseField name="per_page" type="integer" />

  </Expandable>

## Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | An unknown `category` (the five above) |
| `400` | `q` shorter than 2 or longer than 60 characters, or with a character outside letters, digits, spaces and `'` `&` `.` `-` |
| `400` | `grading_company` that isn't 2–10 letters |
| `400` | `min_grade`, `max_grade`, `min_price` or `max_price` that isn't a number |
| `400` | `source` other than `all`, `shop` or `listing`, or `sort` other than `price_asc` or `price_desc` |
| `500` | We couldn't run the search. Retry with backoff |
