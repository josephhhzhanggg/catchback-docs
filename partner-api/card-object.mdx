---
title: "The card object"
description: "One card shape, returned by every endpoint that returns a card."
---

The card catalog, a pack's pool, the cards you pull, and your vault all return the same card object. Fields a card doesn't have are `null`; fields that only apply to one catalog (sports, watches) appear only on cards from that catalog.

## Identity

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">The card's id. Use it for sell-back and listings.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="category" type="string">`pokemon`, `onepiece`, `sports`, `riftbound` or `watches`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="name" type="string">Card name.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="set" type="string">Set name.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="year" type="string">Release year.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="number" type="string">Card number within the set.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="variant" type="string">Print variant. Not on sports cards.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="rarity" type="string">Printed rarity (e.g. "Rare Holo"). Not on sports cards.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="card_type" type="string">Card type. Not on sports cards.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="language" type="string">Card language.</ResponseField>

## Slab

<ResponseField name="slab_label" type="string">The text on the grading label.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="grading_company" type="string">`PSA`, `CGC`, `BGS` and so on.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="grading_rank" type="number">The grade. Half grades are real numbers (`9.5`). `10.5` is CGC Pristine 10.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="cert_number" type="string">The grading company's certificate number, to verify the slab on their site.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="card_condition" type="string">Raw-card condition, where recorded.</ResponseField>

## Value

<ResponseField name="market_value" type="number">Our current market valuation in USD. It drives pack odds and buyback offers.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="price" type="number">The shop sale price in USD.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="market_value_last_updated" type="string">When `market_value` was last refreshed (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>

## Images

<ResponseField name="front_image" type="string">Scan of the front of the slab.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="back_image" type="string">Scan of the back of the slab.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="render_image" type="string">The slab on our display pedestal, as our app shows it.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="image_url" type="string">Same as `render_image`, kept for older integrations.</ResponseField>

<Note>
  Scans can be several MB each. Cache them and resize on your side; don't hotlink full-size images in list views.
</Note>
