Every `/api/partner/v1/` request sends your API key as a bearer token:

```http
Authorization: Bearer cb_live_...
```

A few read-only endpoints take no key: [Public card metadata](/partner-api/endpoints/get-public-card), [Verify a pull](/partner-api/verify-a-pull) and the list of countries we ship to ([Shipping cards](/partner-api/shipping#where-you-can-ship)).

A key is `cb_live_` followed by 48 hexadecimal characters. We store only a hash of it, so we can't show it to you again: keep it safe when you receive it.

A missing or malformed key is a `401` with `"error": "Missing or malformed API key"`. A disabled or unknown key is a `401` with `"error": "Invalid API key"`.

## Scopes

Each key carries a set of scopes, and every endpoint except [Who am I](/partner-api/endpoints/get-me) and [Public card metadata](/partner-api/endpoints/get-public-card) requires exactly one. Calling an endpoint your key has no scope for is a `403` (`"error": "API key lacks required scope: <scope>"`). We grant scopes per key; ask us if you need one you don't have.

| Scope | Lets you | Moves money | Granted |
|---|---|---|---|
| `read:catalog` | See the packs you can buy, what each pack can pull, and every card for sale (shop and user listings); look any card up by id or cert, with its price history and recent sales and listings | No | Standard |
| `read:account` | See your balance, limits, the cards you hold, your own listings and your order history | No | Standard |
| `rip:packs` | Open packs with your CatchCoins | **Yes** | Standard |
| `buy:cards` | Buy cards from the shop and user listings with your CatchCoins | **Yes** | Standard |
| `sell:cards` | Sell cards back to CatchBack for CatchCoins | **Yes** | Standard |
| `write:listings` | List held cards on the marketplace at a fixed price, change a listing's price, and take listings down | No | Standard |
| `ship:cards` | Ship cards you hold to your customers with your CatchCoins, and track those shipments. Each shipment stores its customer's address | **Yes** | **Opt-in** |
| `read:pulls` | Revenue-share report of completed pack purchases | No | **Opt-in** |
| `create:test_accounts` | Create test logins with a test balance, and see the ones you made | No: test balance only | **Opt-in** |

Standard scopes are the usual set for a partner key; we may leave out any you don't need. `ship:cards`, `read:pulls` and `create:test_accounts` are opt-in: they're never on a new key by default, and we grant them on request.

Account actions (`read:account`, `rip:packs`, `buy:cards`, `sell:cards`, `write:listings`, `ship:cards`) also need the key to be linked to a CatchBack account. An unlinked key gets a `403` on them (`"error": "This API key is not linked to a CatchBack account"`). If we can't read the link just then, the answer is the same `403`, so one retry is worth it. [Who am I](/partner-api/endpoints/get-me) shows whether your key is linked, and to which account.

## Keeping your key safe

<Warning>
  A key with `rip:packs`, `buy:cards`, `sell:cards` or `ship:cards` can move your balance. Treat it like a bank credential.
</Warning>

- Keep it **server-side only**. Never put it in browser, mobile or other client code.
- Store it in a secret manager, not in source control.
- Keys don't expire. When someone with access leaves your team, ask us for a new key and we'll disable the old one.
- If it leaks, tell us immediately at [catchbackcards@gmail.com](mailto:catchbackcards@gmail.com). Keys are revoked individually, within minutes, without affecting your other keys.
