## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is CatchBack cards?">
    CatchBack is a digital trading card platform that combines the excitement of pack opening with real-world card ownership. You purchase digital packs, open them to reveal cards, and those collectibles are backed by physical cards stored in our secure vault. You can trade, sell, or request physical delivery of your cards at any time. Users can also create custom packs where they pick their exact hits and odds, and buy and sell cards in our marketplace with just a $1 fee for completed sales.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How does the CatchBack offer work?">
    Every eligible card in your vault carries a standing buyback offer. For the first **5 minutes** after you pull a card from a pack, the offer is **90%**; after that, it's **80%**, with no expiration — hold as long as you like and cash out whenever you choose. For the first **7 days** after you acquire a card (from a pack or a purchase), the offer is based on the card's value at the moment you got it — fixed, whether the market moves up or down. After 7 days it's based on the card's current market value. Your vault always shows the exact dollar offer on each card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do payments and deposits work?">
    You can add funds by card or with crypto (USDC), and established accounts may also be offered Affirm. International customers can pay in their local currency where supported. Deposited funds go toward packs and purchases on CatchBack; money from CatchBacks and marketplace sales can be withdrawn to your bank account, or as USDC to your own crypto wallet, with a $10 minimum. If you deposited by card in the last 60 days, withdrawals up to that amount can only go to a bank.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How do I get started?">
    Simply create an account, add to your balance, and start opening packs. You can begin creating custom packs to chase your exact hits or start with our Starter Pack for just 8 dollars. Your digital cards are immediately added to your vault and ready to trade.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Are my cards safe?">
    Absolutely. Your physical cards are stored in professional-grade facilities with climate control, security monitoring, and full insurance coverage. We use industry-standard security practices and maintain detailed records of every card in our vault. You can request physical delivery of your cards at any time.
  </Accordion>
