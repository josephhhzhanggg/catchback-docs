# Compliance & Regulations

CatchBack works to comply with the laws and regulations that apply to our platform in the places we operate. This page summarizes the controls that protect your account, your money and your data.

## Eligibility

- You must be at least 18 years old to use CatchBack.
- CatchBack isn't available in countries subject to comprehensive sanctions, and access from those countries is blocked.
- Deposits, shipping and withdrawals are available in the US and a set of supported countries. Text Sona at +1 (645) 232-0590 to confirm whether yours is supported.

## Payments

- **PCI-compliant payment processing**: Card payments are handled by established, PCI DSS-compliant payment processors. We don't store your full card details on our own systems.
- **Identity verification**: You may be asked to verify your identity before withdrawing.
- **Phone verification**: A verified phone number is required for free rewards, withdrawals, trades, gifts, pack drops and some other features.
- **Withdrawal review**: Some withdrawals are reviewed by our team before they are sent. A withdrawal under review shows as **in review**.

## Fraud Prevention

To protect the platform and its users, we monitor for fraud and abuse, including multiple accounts, self-referral and payment fraud. Accounts found violating our [Terms of Service](/logistics-legal/terms) may have rewards reversed, withdrawals held, or access restricted.

## Data Protection

- Our [Privacy Policy](/logistics-legal/privacy) explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have, including for tracking and advertising.
- We keep records of purchases, deposits, withdrawals, trades and shipments for at least 7 years, as financial-services, tax and anti-fraud law requires. See [Account Deletion](/logistics-legal/account-deletion).

## Text Messages

When you text our support line, you agree to receive customer support text messages from CatchBack Cards Inc. This does not sign you up for marketing texts. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for help.

## Security Practices

We follow industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit, access controls and audit logging, and we continually review our security posture as the platform grows.
