# Deleting Your Account

You can permanently close your CatchBack account and request deletion of your data at any time, from the app or from the website.

## Deletion Methods

### Option 1: Delete Through the App

1. Open the CatchBack app
2. Open the three-line menu at the top left and go to **Account**
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm twice. Your account is closed immediately

### Option 2: Delete on the Website

This works whether or not you have the app installed:

1. Go to [catchbackcards.com/delete-account](https://catchbackcards.com/delete-account)
2. Sign in to the account you want to delete
3. Type the confirmation word shown on the page and press **Delete my account**

We only delete an account for someone signed in to it, so we can be sure it's yours. If you can't sign in, email [support@catchbackcards.com](mailto:support@catchbackcards.com) and we'll help. We respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

## Important Information

<Warning>
**Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.**

Once your account is deleted:
- You can no longer sign in, and a closed account can't be merged into a new one
- You lose access to everything held in the account, including your card collection, your CatchCoins balance and any store credit
- Any active marketplace listings are cancelled
</Warning>

### Before You Delete

Please make sure you:
- Withdraw any balance you can withdraw
- Request shipping for any cards you want to keep
- Save any information you may need for future reference

## What Is Deleted, and What We Keep

When your account is deleted we close it, revoke your sign-in credentials, cancel your active listings, and stop all marketing and notification messages. Your profile is no longer usable by anyone, including you.

We keep a record of your purchases, deposits, withdrawals, marketplace trades and shipments, together with the identifying details attached to them (name, email, phone and shipping address). Financial-services, tax and anti-fraud law require us to retain these, and we hold them for at least 7 years from the date of each transaction. They are not used for marketing or shared for advertising, and we don't keep them longer than the law requires.
